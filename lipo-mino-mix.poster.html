What is LIPO-Mino-Mix?

LIPO-Mino-Mix is a pre-mixed "lipotropic" injection (not listed on the catalog page). These nutrients help the liver move fat, but no randomized trial shows the injections cause weight loss beyond diet. The catalog doesn't state how much of each ingredient is in the vial.
