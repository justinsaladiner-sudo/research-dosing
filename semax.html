Semax

ACTH(4–7) analog heptapeptide

Catalog: Semax · Neuropeptides and cognitive references · Evidence ceiling A

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • DRaises BDNF; in rats, intranasal Semax increased hippocampal BDNF protein up to 1.4-fold. Melanocortin-system modulation is also proposed.1

Identity

ClassSynthetic heptapeptide: ACTH(4–7) plus a Pro-Gly-Pro tail for stability
Regulatory statusApproved in Russia and some CIS countries as a nootropic / neuroprotective (intranasal), including stroke recovery. Not FDA-approved; not evaluated by Western regulators.
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 10 mg vial for injection (catalog).

Intended effects

  • AStroke recovery, cognitive impairment (Russian approval, intranasal)1
  • EFocus / cognition (community use)2

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • CGusev 1997 (non-randomized): 30 acute-stroke patients given Semax vs 80 conventional-therapy controls. Recovery effect reported without effect sizes or p-values.1
    • CThe evidence is mostly from Russian institutions in Russian-language journals; independent Western replication is essentially absent.1
    • N/ENo data for SC injection; the approved route is intranasal.1

    Preclinical findings

    • DRat studies: BDNF increase in the hippocampus.1

    Documented dosing & cycles

    E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)2

    • 10 mg + 3 mL BAC water
    • Weeks 1–2: 10–15 units (≈0.33–0.5 mg) → weeks 3–4: 15 units → weeks 5–8: 30 units (1 mg), once daily, 5–7 days/week
    • Morning; 6–8 week cycle, then 6–8 week washout
    • Nasal instructions 'coming soon'

    Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

    Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

    Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

    Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.
