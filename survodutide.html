06

Survodutide

Dual GLP-1 / glucagon receptor agonist

Catalog: Survodutide · Metabolic compounds · Evidence ceiling B

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • BGLP-1R drives satiety and insulin secretion. The glucagon-receptor arm targets energy expenditure and liver fat, which is why it's also being developed for MASH.2

Identity

AliasesBI 456906 (Boehringer Ingelheim / Zealand Pharma)
ClassDual GLP-1 / glucagon receptor agonist (peptide)
TargetsGLP-1 receptor · Glucagon receptor
Regulatory statusInvestigational. Phase 3 SYNCHRONIZE-1 and -2 positive in 2026.
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 10 mg vial (catalog).

Intended effects

  • BWeight management1
  • BLiver fat / MASH2

Secondary findings

  • BSYNCHRONIZE-1 substudy: up to −34% visceral fat and up to −63.1% liver fat.2

Human evidence

  • BPhase 2 (n=386, no diabetes, 46 wk, planned treatment): −6.2% (0.6 mg), −12.5% (2.4 mg), −13.2% (3.6 mg), −14.9% (4.8 mg) vs −2.8% placebo. 18.7% at 4.8 mg is the actual-treatment analysis.1
  • BPhase 2: adverse-event discontinuation 25% (pooled survodutide, mostly GI) vs 4% placebo. Fast escalation was a factor.1
  • BSYNCHRONIZE-1 (no diabetes, 76 wk, 3.6/6.0 mg): up to −16.6% vs −3.2% placebo (efficacy estimand). Published NEJM 2026.2
  • BSYNCHRONIZE-2 (T2D, 76 wk): up to −13.1% vs −3.1%; HbA1c down up to 1.21%.3

    E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)4

    • Start at 1.6 mg; stay at least 4 weeks before increasing
    • Raise in 0.8 mg steps; maximum 6.4 mg
    • Once weekly, same day each week
    • 4.8 mg flagged as 'the clinical trial dose for 19% weight loss'

    Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

    Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

    • Catalog maximum 6.4 mg is above the highest phase 3 dose (6.0 mg).
    • The catalog's '19% at 4.8 mg' is the actual-treatment analysis. The planned-treatment result was −14.9%.
    • Unit math: every unit value the catalog prints matches (6.67 mg/mL).

    Optimization & support

    Foundational support

    CProtein 1.2–1.6 g/kg/d during active loss6
    Why
    GLP-1-class drugs cut energy intake by 16–39%, so lean mass is at risk.
    Mechanism
    Supplies amino acids for muscle protein synthesis during an energy deficit. Base the target on adjusted body weight or fat-free mass, not actual weight. Avoid ≥2 g/kg/d long term.
    Basis
    Inferred: joint advisory for GLP-1 therapy. Not tested with this compound.
    Downside
    Protein above muscle needs may convert to fat. Protein/fat-heavy meals slow gastric emptying and can worsen constipation.
    BResistance training ≥3×/wk + ≥150 min/wk aerobic6
    Why
    Protein alone is unlikely to preserve muscle without structured strength training.
    Mechanism
    Mechanical loading signals muscle to keep lean and bone mass during rapid weight loss. Aerobic work alone preserves less.
    Basis
    Inferred: weight-loss trials + GLP-1 retrospective data via the joint advisory.
    Downside
    None specific. Ramp up gradually if intake is very low.
    CHydration + electrolytes6
    Why
    Nausea, vomiting and diarrhea are common with incretin drugs.
    Mechanism
    GI fluid loss leads to volume depletion. Postmarket GLP-1 reports link this to renal impairment around initiation and dose escalation.
    Basis
    Inferred: GLP-1 class postmarket reports via the advisory.
    Downside
    Watch renal function if renal impairment is present.
    CMicronutrient sufficiency (consider vitamin D, calcium, B12, multivitamin-mineral)6
    Why
    At intakes under ≈1,200 kcal/d (women) or 1,800 kcal/d (men), deficiencies become likely.
    Mechanism
    Nutrients of concern: iron, calcium, magnesium, zinc, and vitamins A, D, E, K, B1, B12, C. Warning signs: unusual fatigue, hair loss, poor wound healing.
    Basis
    Inferred: GLP-1 class advisory.
    Downside
    Dose supplements to need. Fat-soluble vitamins can accumulate.

    Mechanistic support

    CFiber, magnesium citrate, PEG 3350 for constipation6
    Why
    Constipation is a common on-target effect of GLP-1 agonism.
    Mechanism
    Slower GI transit. Fiber adds bulk. Magnesium and PEG draw water into the stool osmotically.
    Basis
    Inferred: advisory management guidance for GLP-1 drugs.
    Downside
    Magnesium can cause diarrhea or build up when kidney function is reduced.
    CSmall, low-fat meals; stop eating at satiety6
    Why
    Reduces nausea and diarrhea, which are worse with large or high-fat meals.
    Mechanism
    Delayed gastric emptying plus a large gastric load causes distension and nausea.
    Basis
    Inferred: advisory guidance.
    Downside
    Watch that protein targets are still met.

    Complementary interventions

    BCreatine monohydrate with resistance training7
    Why
    Helps protect lean mass while drug-driven weight loss strips muscle as well as fat.
    Mechanism
    Raises muscle creatine stores, supporting training volume and lean-mass gains.
    Basis
    Strong evidence for lean mass with resistance training (ISSN 2017). Not tested together with GLP-1 drugs; inferred.
    Downside
    Water weight gain (scale noise); caution with existing kidney disease.

    A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

    Redundancies & overlap

    MazdutideSame GLP-1/glucagon dual mechanism.
    Retatrutide (GLP3-R)Same two receptors plus GIP.
    Semaglutide, tirzepatideOverlap at GLP-1R. The catalog says alternating 'prevents GLP-1 receptor desensitization'; that's untested.
    Catalog stacks (glutathione + NAD+, AOD-9604, CJC/ipamorelin)No combination data for any of them.

    Specific cautions

    • BGI intolerance led to 25% discontinuation in phase 2. Slower escalation matters.1
    • AGLP-1 class warnings apply: MTC/MEN 2, pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, hypoglycemia with insulin/sulfonylureas.5
    • CGlucagon-receptor agonism is associated with heart-rate increases in this drug class. Survodutide-specific heart-rate data weren't reviewed this wave.5

    Research gaps

    • Dose-specific SYNCHRONIZE results (3.6 vs 6.0 mg) and the escalation schedule.
    • Cardiovascular outcomes.
    • Doses above 6.0 mg (the catalog goes to 6.4).
