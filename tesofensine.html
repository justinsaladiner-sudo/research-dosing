Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • BPhase 2 (Astrup 2008, 203 adults, 24 wk): placebo-subtracted weight loss 4.5% / 9.2% / 10.6% at 0.25 / 0.5 / 1 mg. The Lancet issued an expression of concern on the paper in 2013.2
    • BRaised heart rate (up to ~8 bpm) and blood pressure slightly; possible psychiatric effects; lean-mass loss alongside fat.2
    • CMexico approval: conflicting. The company said in Nov 2024 it was NOT yet approved.3

      Complementary interventions

      BCreatine monohydrate with resistance training5
      Why
      Helps protect lean mass while drug-driven weight loss strips muscle as well as fat.
      Mechanism
      Raises muscle creatine stores, supporting training volume and lean-mass gains.
      Basis
      Strong evidence for lean mass with resistance training (ISSN 2017). Not tested together with GLP-1 drugs; inferred.
      Downside
      Water weight gain (scale noise); caution with existing kidney disease.

      A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

      Specific cautions

      • ASerotonergic: combining with methylene blue, MAOIs or other serotonergic drugs risks serotonin syndrome (methylene blue label lists SNRIs).4
