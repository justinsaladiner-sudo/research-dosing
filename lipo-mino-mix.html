LIPO-Mino-Mix

Lipotropic (MIC-type) injection blend

Catalog: LIPO-Mino-Mix · Lipotropics · Evidence ceiling C

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • CLipotropic ingredients (methionine, inositol, choline, carnitine) take part in liver fat export and fatty-acid transport. That's a nutritional role, not evidence that injecting them burns fat.2

Identity

Ingredients (catalog)Not listed on the catalog page
ClassPre-mixed amino acid / vitamin "lipotropic" injection
Regulatory statusNot an approved drug product; a compounded / research blend.
Supplier formulationPre-made solution, dosed by volume. Vial size not stated. The catalog page is a stub.

Intended effects

  • EFat loss / energy (community and clinic marketing)1

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • CNo randomized trials show lipotropic (MIC) injections cause weight loss beyond diet. Ingredient evidence is indirect (e.g. choline reducing liver fat during calorie restriction).2

    Preclinical findings

      Documented dosing & cycles

      E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)1

      • 0.5–1 mL (50–100 units) once daily, 2–3 days a week
      • or 0.5–1.5 mL (50–150 units), 3–5 days a week
      • Morning; no fasting
      • 8+ week cycle, then a washout at least half as long

      Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

      Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

      • The catalog writes doses as "50–100 units (500–1,000 mcg)" without saying which ingredient the mcg refers to.
      • Vial size and per-ingredient concentrations aren't stated.
      • The catalog page has no ingredient list.

      Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

      Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.

      Optimization & support

      Foundational support

      B150–300 min/wk moderate aerobic + strength work ≥2 days/wk3
      Why
      The proven fat-loss and metabolic-health lever, whatever compound is added.
      Mechanism
      Raises energy expenditure, improves insulin sensitivity and preserves lean mass.
      Basis
      General US guideline, not tested with this compound. Any 'exercise-mimetic' claim for a compound is no substitute.
      Downside
      None specific.
      CProtein 1.2–1.6 g/kg/d during active weight reduction4
      Why
      Protects lean mass during an energy deficit.
      Mechanism
      Supplies amino acids for muscle protein synthesis. Base the target on adjusted body weight or fat-free mass. Avoid ≥2 g/kg/d long term.
      Basis
      Inferred: weight-reduction targets cited in the 2025 joint advisory. Not tested with this compound.
      Downside
      Excess protein beyond muscle needs isn't stored as muscle.

      Complementary interventions
