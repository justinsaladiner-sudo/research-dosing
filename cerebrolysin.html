Cerebrolysin

Porcine brain-derived peptide mixture

Catalog: Cerebrolysin · Neuropeptides and cognitive references · Evidence ceiling A

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • CProposed neurotrophic action (described as mimicking factors such as NGF/CNTF), supporting neuron survival and plasticity. The mixture's composition makes its mechanism hard to pin down.1

Identity

ClassMixture of low-molecular-weight peptides and amino acids from pig brain (EVER Neuro Pharma)
Regulatory statusApproved and marketed in many countries outside the US (Austrian marketing authorization) for dementia, stroke and brain trauma. Not FDA-registered.
Approved formSolution for injection/infusion, 215.2 mg/mL concentrate, in ampoules. Given IV (or IM) in milliliters.
Supplier formulationSold as a powder: 8 mL mix, '100 units = 8 mg', which implies about 64 mg per vial. That's a different form from the approved product.

Intended effects

  • AAlzheimer's and vascular dementia, stroke, traumatic brain injury (approved label, where marketed)1
  • ECognition / neuroprotection (community use)3

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • ACochrane (acute ischemic stroke): probably no effect on all-cause death (moderate certainty).2
    • AThe same review: total serious adverse events probably not different (RR 1.16), but non-fatal serious adverse events increased (RR 2.39), more so with 30 mL × 10 days (RR 2.87).2
    • AMost included trials were supplied and designed by the manufacturer (conflict of interest).2

    Preclinical findings

      Documented dosing & cycles

      A Clinical dosing (approved solution)2

      30 mL IVDaily × 10 days (stroke trials)

      At 215.2 mg/mL, 30 mL is about 6.5 g of concentrate per day, IV. The catalog's 8–24 mg SC is roughly 0.1–0.4% of that, in a different form.

      E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)3

      • Mix with 8 mL BAC water (100 units = 8 mg)
      • Week 1: 8 mg once daily; week 2: twice daily; weeks 3–4: three times daily
      • 4-week cycle, then 2–4 week washout
      • Morning or early afternoon

      Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

      Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

      • Form: the approved Cerebrolysin is a 215.2 mg/mL solution. The catalog sells a powder at about 8 mg/mL after mixing.
      • Dose: clinical use is milliliters IV (e.g. 30 mL/day); the catalog's 8–24 mg/day SC is a tiny fraction.
      • Vial size isn't stated; ≈64 mg is implied by '8 mL, 100 units = 8 mg'.

      Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

      Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.
