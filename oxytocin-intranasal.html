Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • BNasal is the main research route. The largest trial (Sikich 2021, autism, up to 48 IU/day, 24 weeks) found no benefit over placebo.2
    • N/EResearch doses are given in IU; the mcg-to-IU conversion wasn't verified, so the catalog dose can't be compared directly.2

    Preclinical findings

      Documented dosing & cycles

      E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)1

      • Up to 3 sprays (150 mcg) daily, in 1–2 doses
      • 4–12 week cycle, then 2–6 week washout

      Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

      Product & dosing units (Intranasal)

      • Nasal spray
      • 1 spray = 50 mcg

      No syringe math applies to this route.
