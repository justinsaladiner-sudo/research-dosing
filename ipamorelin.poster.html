What is Ipamorelin?

Ipamorelin is a small peptide that activates the ghrelin receptor and triggers a pulse of growth hormone. It was tested in humans only for bowel recovery after surgery, and that trial missed its goal. Its body-composition use is untested.

Clinical evidence

  • BPhase 2 (n=114 bowel-resection patients, dosed from postoperative day 1 to day 7): time to a solid meal 25.3 h vs 32.6 h placebo, not significant (p=0.15)
  • N/ENo human trials of subcutaneous ipamorelin for body composition.

Common side effects

  • Adverse events similar to placebo in the ileus trial
  • Hunger, water retention (class, anecdotal)
  • Injection-site reactions

Important considerations

  • Raised GH/IGF-1: glucose intolerance, fluid retention (class analogy)
  • No long-term human safety data for repeated SC dosing

Key notes (catalog)

  • 100–300 mcg per dose, 1–3 times a day
  • Bedtime; add on waking; add within 30 min post-workout
  • Fasted (≥1.5 h before/after a meal)
  • 8–12 week cycle, then 4–6 week washout

Reconstitution example

Add 2.5 mL BAC water → 4 mg/mL

  1. Swab both vial tops with an alcohol pad
  2. Draw the BAC water (3 mL syringe, 22–23G)
  3. Inject slowly into the peptide vial
  4. Roll gently between your palms; don't shake
  5. Let it sit 15–30 min until fully dissolved

Catalog: refrigerate reconstituted vials in an opaque, airtight container, not in the door. Use within 60–90 days of mixing (catalog claim, not stability data). Powder: room temperature 30–60 days, fridge up to 1 year.
