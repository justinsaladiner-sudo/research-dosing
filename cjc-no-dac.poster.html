What is CJC-1295 (no DAC)?

CJC-1295 without DAC (also called modified GRF 1–29) is a breakdown-resistant version of sermorelin that gives a short pulse of growth hormone. The long-acting DAC version reached human trials. This no-DAC form never did.

Clinical evidence

  • N/ENo human trials of the no-DAC form were found.
  • BThe DAC form (a different molecule): single doses in healthy adults raised GH and IGF-1 for 9–11 days; half-life 5.8–8.1 days; no serious adverse reactions.
  • CThe DAC form's phase 2 in HIV visceral obesity (192 enrolled) was halted in July 2006 after a participant died

Common side effects

  • No human data for this form
  • Flushing, head rush (GHRH class, anecdotal)
  • Water retention at higher GH

Important considerations

  • No human safety data for this molecule
  • Raised GH/IGF-1: glucose intolerance, fluid retention (class analogy)
  • Don't confuse with CJC-1295 DAC: it lasts days, not minutes, so dosing isn't interchangeable

Key notes (catalog)

  • Once daily: 100 mcg (weeks 1–4), then 150 mcg (weeks 5–12)
  • Twice daily: 100 → 200 → 300 mcg; three times daily up to 500 mcg
  • Fasted 2–3 hours after eating; usually stacked with ipamorelin
  • 12-week cycle, then 6–12 week washout

Reconstitution example

Add 2.5 mL BAC water → 4 mg/mL

  1. Swab both vial tops with an alcohol pad
  2. Draw the BAC water (3 mL syringe, 22–23G)
  3. Inject slowly into the peptide vial
  4. Roll gently between your palms; don't shake
  5. Let it sit 15–30 min until fully dissolved

Catalog: refrigerate reconstituted vials in an opaque, airtight container, not in the door. Use within 60–90 days of mixing (catalog claim, not stability data). Powder: room temperature 30–60 days, fridge up to 1 year.
