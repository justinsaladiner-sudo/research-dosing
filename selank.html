    Documented dosing & cycles

    E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)2

    • 10 mg + 2.5 mL BAC water; use 30-unit syringes
    • 250–300 mcg once daily in the morning, or 250–500 mcg twice daily
    • Fasted when possible
    • 4–6 week cycle, then 2–4 week washout

    Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

    Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

    • At 4 mg/mL (40 mcg/unit), the catalog's units run short: 350 mcg = 8.75 u (catalog 8), 400 = 10 (9), 450 = 11.25 (10), 500 = 12.5 (11).
    • 250 and 300 mcg match within rounding.

    Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

    Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.

    Catalog math mismatches: 10 mg vial, 0.35 mg: catalog says 8 units, math says 8.8; 10 mg vial, 0.4 mg: catalog says 9 units, math says 10; 10 mg vial, 0.45 mg: catalog says 10 units, math says 11.3; 10 mg vial, 0.5 mg: catalog says 11 units, math says 12.5
