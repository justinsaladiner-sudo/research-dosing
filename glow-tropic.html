      Documented dosing & cycles

      E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)1

      • Pre-made: per mL GHK-Cu 2 mg, glutathione 200 mg, histidine 100 mg, glycine 50 mg, NADH 50 mg
      • Week 1: 25 then 50 units; week 2: 75 + 75 units; weeks 3–6: 100 + 100 units
      • Twice a week, 3–4 days apart
      • 4–6 week cycle, then 2–3 week washout

      Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

      Optimization & support

      Foundational support

      AVitamin C sufficiency (RDA 75 mg women / 90 mg men)3
      Why
      Collagen can't be made properly without vitamin C.
      Mechanism
      Cofactor for prolyl and lysyl hydroxylases that stabilize collagen.
      Basis
      Established nutrition science (NIH ODS). Extra beyond sufficiency isn't shown to boost repair-peptide effects.
      Downside
      Very high doses can cause GI upset and raise kidney-stone risk in susceptible people (NIH ODS).

      Mechanistic support

      BHydrolyzed collagen peptides daily4
      Why
      The best-supported oral supplement for skin hydration and elasticity.
      Mechanism
      Collagen-derived peptides (e.g. Pro-Hyp) are absorbed and signal fibroblasts; also supply amino acids for matrix.
      Basis
      Meta-analyses of 19–26 RCTs: better hydration and elasticity; wrinkle reduction not robust. Not tested with GHK-Cu or other peptides.
      Downside
      Benefit builds over weeks; duration mattered more than dose.

      Complementary interventions

      BGlycine + N-acetylcysteine (GlyNAC)5
      Why
      Supplies the rate-limiting building blocks the body uses to make its own glutathione, by mouth.
      Mechanism
      Cysteine (from NAC) and glycine are glutathione precursors.
      Basis
      A 16-week RCT in older adults raised glutathione; a larger 2-week dose trial didn't overall (only in people starting low). Mixed. Trial doses were weight-based.
      Downside
      NAC: GI upset; interacts with nitroglycerin (headache/hypotension).

      A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.
