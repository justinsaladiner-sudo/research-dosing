What is Tesamorelin?

Tesamorelin is a stabilized form of growth-hormone-releasing hormone. It makes the pituitary release more of its own GH in natural pulses. It's FDA-approved (Egrifta) to cut abdominal visceral fat in people with HIV-associated lipodystrophy.

Clinical evidence

  • ATwo 26-week phase 3 trials (HIV lipodystrophy, 2 mg daily): visceral fat −18% vs +2% placebo (study 1) and −14% vs −2% (study 2).
  • ALong-term cardiovascular safety not established (label).
  • AThe approved population is HIV lipodystrophy

Common side effects

  • Injection-site reactions
  • Arthralgia, peripheral edema, carpal tunnel (fluid retention)
  • Raised glucose / HbA1c
  • Raised IGF-1

Important considerations

  • Contraindicated: disrupted hypothalamic-pituitary axis, active malignancy, pregnancy
  • Elevated IGF-1: monitor it; effects of prolonged elevation unknown
  • Glucose intolerance or diabetes may develop: check glucose before and during
  • Fluid retention: edema, joint pain, carpal tunnel

Key notes (catalog)

Reconstitution example

Add 3 mL acetic acid + BAC water → 3.3 mg/mL

  1. Bring the vial, acetic acid and BAC water to room temperature
  2. Add the acetic acid slowly down the vial wall; let it dissolve
  3. Then add the BAC water the same way
  4. Roll gently; don't shake
  5. Let it sit until fully clear

Catalog: refrigerate reconstituted vials in an opaque, airtight container, not in the door. Use within 60–90 days of mixing (catalog claim, not stability data). Powder: room temperature 30–60 days, fridge up to 1 year.
