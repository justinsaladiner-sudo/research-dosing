What is Mazdutide?

Mazdutide is a once-weekly dual GLP-1 and glucagon receptor agonist modeled on the gut hormone oxyntomodulin. China approved it for weight management in June 2025, the first drug of its class approved anywhere.

Clinical evidence

  • BGLORY-1 (n=610 Chinese adults, 48 wk): −12.05% (4 mg), −14.84% (6 mg) vs −0.47% placebo

Common side effects

  • GI effects are expected (GLP-1 class)
  • Rates weren't pulled from GLORY-1 this wave

Important considerations

  • GLP-1 class warnings apply: MTC/MEN 2, pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, hypoglycemia with insulin/sulfonylureas
  • Glucagon-receptor agonism raised heart rate with retatrutide

Key notes (catalog)

Reconstitution example

Add 1 mL BAC water → 10 mg/mL

  1. Swab both vial tops with an alcohol pad
  2. Draw the BAC water (3 mL syringe, 22–23G)
  3. Inject slowly into the peptide vial
  4. Roll gently between your palms; don't shake
  5. Let it sit 15–30 min until fully dissolved

Catalog: refrigerate reconstituted vials in an opaque, airtight container, not in the door. Use within 60–90 days of mixing (catalog claim, not stability data). Powder: room temperature 30–60 days, fridge up to 1 year.

Common combinations

  • Creatine monohydrate with resistance training B
  • Protein 1.2–1.6 g/kg/d during active loss C
  • Resistance training ≥3×/wk + ≥150 min/wk aerobic B

Avoid / use caution with

  • Personal or family history of medullary thyroid cancer or MEN 2 (class)
  • Insulin or sulfonylureas (hypoglycemia)
  • Other GLP-1 / glucagon agonists
  • History of pancreatitis

Similar options

  • Survodutide: The same GLP-1/glucagon dual mechanism; a direct alternative
  • Retatrutide (GLP3-R): Adds GIP on top of the same two receptors
  • Semaglutide, tirzepatide: Overlap at GLP-1R

Research gaps

  • Escalation schedule and adverse-event rates from the GLORY-1 paper (not pulled this wave).
  • Data outside Chinese populations.
  • Long-term cardiovascular and heart-rate effects.
  • Research powder vs the approved Chinese product.
