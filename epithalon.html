Epitalon

Pineal tetrapeptide (Ala-Glu-Asp-Gly)

Catalog: Epithalon · Bioregulators · Evidence ceiling C

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • DClaimed to activate telomerase. Evidence is in vitro (human fibroblast cultures), not human telomere trials.1
  • DClaimed to restore melatonin rhythm. One study found no melatonin stimulation in isolated rat pineal glands.1

Identity

AliasesEpithalon, Epitalon, AEDG; synthetic version of the pineal extract epithalamin
ClassKhavinson tetrapeptide bioregulator, sequence Ala-Glu-Asp-Gly
Regulatory statusNot FDA-approved. Compounding status reportedly changed in 2026 (single secondary source; unverified).
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 10 mg and 50 mg vials (catalog).

Intended effects

  • EAnti-aging, sleep / circadian rhythm (developer and community claims)2

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • CThe mortality claim (≈266 elderly people; 1.6–1.8× lower mortality) comes from non-randomized, non-blinded programs run by the developers. Reported durations conflict (6–8 vs 12 years), and much of it used the extract epithalamin, not synthetic Epitalon.1
    • CNo independent Western replication of any human finding.1

    Preclinical findings

    • DLifespan extension reported in flies (≈11–16%) and rodents (Anisimov et al.).1

    Documented dosing & cycles

    E Catalog protocols (researchdosing.com)2

    • 'Ukrainian' protocol: 10 mg on days 1, 5, 9, 13, 17 (recommended first cycle)
    • Standard: 5 mg daily for 20 days (10 mg vial) or 10 days (50 mg vial)
    • Microdose: 100 mcg daily for 6 weeks (10 mg in 3 mL)
    • Maximum: 10 mg daily for 10 days
    • Repeat 2–3 times per year

    Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

    Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

    • The catalog's '28% lower rate' claim from a 12-year follow-up conflicts with other accounts of the same program (6–8 years; 1.6–1.8× lower mortality).
    • Unit math: every unit value the catalog prints matches across all three mixes.

    Optimization & support

    Foundational support

    BProtect slow-wave sleep3
    Why
    The largest natural GH pulse comes in early slow-wave sleep. That's the physiology bedtime GH-secretagogue dosing tries to ride.
    Mechanism
    Slow-wave sleep and nocturnal GH release are tightly coupled. Both decline with age.
    Basis
    Inferred: GH physiology. Not tested as an add-on to this compound.
    Downside
    None.

    Mechanistic support

    Complementary interventions

    BMelatonin before bed4
    Why
    The evidence-backed sleep-onset aid, for compounds marketed for sleep.
    Mechanism
    MT1/MT2 receptor agonist; shifts and signals the circadian night.
    Basis
    Meta-analysis of 19 RCTs: falls asleep ~7 min faster; larger effects with higher dose and longer use. Not tested with DSIP, Pinealon or Epitalon.
    Downside
    Modest effect; next-day grogginess possible; stacking several sleep agents compounds sedation.

    A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

    Redundancies & overlap

    Pinealon and other Khavinson bioregulatorsSame research source and concept.
    FOXO4-DRIAlso sold for aging; unrelated mechanism; mouse data only.

    Specific cautions

    • DTelomerase activation is the selling point, and also a theoretical concern with existing cancers (inferred; untested).1
    • EThe catalog lists injection-site redness, swelling or itching (resolving within 3 days).2

    Research gaps

    • Any randomized, independent human trial.
    • Synthetic Epitalon (vs the extract) in humans.
