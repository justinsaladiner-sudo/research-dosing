    • BGHRH-type + ghrelin-receptor (GHRP-type) peptides given together produce a strikingly synergistic acute GH release in humans (single-dose physiology studies). That is not evidence for the catalog's months-long blend regimen.3

    Preclinical findings

      Documented dosing & cycles

      E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)1

      • 0.5 mL acetic acid, then 2.5 mL BAC water
      • 23 units at bedtime, 5 days a week (weeks 1–6)
      • Weeks 7–12: stay at 23 or go to 46 units
      • 12–16 week cycle, then 4–8 week washout

      Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

      Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

      • Units only. Computed: 23 units = 0.77 mg tesamorelin + 0.23 mg ipamorelin; 46 units = 1.53 mg + 0.46 mg.
      • The label tesamorelin dose is 1.28–1.4 mg daily.

      Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

      Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.
