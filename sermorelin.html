Sermorelin

GHRH(1–29) analog

Catalog: Sermorelin · Growth hormone axis · Evidence ceiling A

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • AActs on the pituitary GHRH receptor and raises endogenous, pulsatile GH, with somatostatin feedback intact. Short half-life (minutes).1

Identity

AliasesGHRH(1–29)NH2, GRF 1–29 · former brand Geref (EMD Serono)
ClassTruncated GHRH analog (first 29 amino acids)
TargetsPituitary GHRH receptor
Regulatory statusGeref was FDA-approved in 1997 for idiopathic GH deficiency in children with growth failure, plus a diagnostic use. Discontinued 2008; FDA found it was not withdrawn for safety or effectiveness reasons. Today it's available only compounded.
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 5 mg and 10 mg vials (catalog).

Intended effects

  • AGrowth failure in children with idiopathic GH deficiency (former approved use)1

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • AApproved and marketed (Geref) for pediatric GH deficiency until 2008. Its adult anti-aging and body-composition use is off-label and lacks outcome trials.1

    Preclinical findings

      Documented dosing & cycles

      E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)2

      • Ladder 200 → 300 → 400 → 500 mcg once daily, 4 weeks per step (alt. ladder 300 → 600 mcg)
      • Dose at bedtime, fasted (≥1.5 h after the last meal)
      • 12–16 week cycle, then 6–8 week washout

      Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

      Optimization & support

      Foundational support

      BProtect slow-wave sleep4
      Why
      The largest natural GH pulse comes in early slow-wave sleep. That's the physiology bedtime GH-secretagogue dosing tries to ride.
      Mechanism
      Slow-wave sleep and nocturnal GH release are tightly coupled. Both decline with age.
      Basis
      Inferred: GH physiology. Not tested as an add-on to this compound.
      Downside
      None.
      B150–300 min/wk moderate aerobic + strength work ≥2 days/wk5
      Why
      The proven fat-loss and metabolic-health lever, whatever compound is added.
      Mechanism
      Raises energy expenditure, improves insulin sensitivity and preserves lean mass.
      Basis
      General US guideline, not tested with this compound. Any 'exercise-mimetic' claim for a compound is no substitute.
      Downside
      None specific.

      Mechanistic support

      BDose away from carbohydrate / high-glucose meals6
      Why
      Raised glucose and insulin blunt GH release, which is why the catalog says to dose fasted.
      Mechanism
      Oral glucose suppresses GH secretion; acromegaly is diagnosed by the absence of that suppression.
      Basis
      Inferred: GH physiology. The catalog's specific fasting windows are untested.
      Downside
      Hypoglycemia risk if combined with insulin-lowering drugs while fasted.
      AMonitor fasting glucose / HbA1c3
      Why
      GH and IGF-1 raise insulin resistance.
      Mechanism
      GH counters insulin action. In tesamorelin trials HbA1c ≥6.5% developed in 5% vs 1% on placebo.
      Basis
      Demonstrated for tesamorelin (label); applied by analogy to other GH secretagogues.
      Downside
      None.

      Complementary interventions

      A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

      Redundancies & overlap

      Tesamorelin, CJC-1295 (no DAC)Same GHRH receptor. Tesamorelin is the stabilized, still-approved version.
      Ipamorelin, GHRP-2, GHRP-6Different receptor (GHS-R). Often paired; no outcome trial tests the pairing.

      Specific cautions

      • AGH/IGF-1 elevation: the same theoretical concerns as tesamorelin (glucose intolerance, fluid retention, malignancy growth). By class analogy.3
      • ACompounded and research products aren't FDA-checked for quality.1

      Research gaps

      • Adult outcome trials.
      • The verified Geref label dose (not retrieved).
      • The catalog's cycling.
