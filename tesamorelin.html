Tesamorelin

GHRH analog

Catalog: Tesamorelin · Growth hormone axis · Evidence ceiling A

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • ABinds pituitary GHRH receptors and raises GH release, which keeps the normal pulsatile pattern and feedback, unlike exogenous GH. Raises IGF-1.2

Identity

AliasesEgrifta SV, Egrifta WR (approved products)
ClassStabilized GHRH(1–44) analog (trans-3-hexenoyl modification)
TargetsPituitary GHRH receptor, which drives endogenous pulsatile GH and then IGF-1
Regulatory statusFDA-approved to reduce excess abdominal fat in adults with HIV-associated lipodystrophy. Egrifta WR (2025) is replacing Egrifta SV. The catalog powder is not these products.
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 10 mg and 20 mg vials, dissolved in acetic acid and then BAC water (catalog).

Intended effects

  • AVisceral (abdominal) fat reduction in HIV lipodystrophy2

Secondary findings

  • AHbA1c ≥6.5% developed in 5% vs 1% on placebo by week 26.2
  • AFluid retention: edema, arthralgia, carpal tunnel syndrome.2
  • AAnti-tesamorelin antibodies in 56% at 26 weeks, without loss of the visceral-fat or IGF-1 response.2

Human evidence

  • ATwo 26-week phase 3 trials (HIV lipodystrophy, 2 mg daily): visceral fat −18% vs +2% placebo (study 1) and −14% vs −2% (study 2).2
  • ALong-term cardiovascular safety not established (label).2
  • AThe approved population is HIV lipodystrophy. Use for general body composition or anti-aging is off-label and unstudied.2

Preclinical findings

    Documented dosing & cycles

    A Label dosing2

    1.28 mgDaily (Egrifta WR)
    →
    1.4 mgDaily (Egrifta SV)
    →
    2 mgDaily (original formulation, trials)

    Once daily into the abdomen, rotating sites. No titration. The pivotal trials used 2 mg/day.

    E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)1

    • 10 mg vial: 0.5 mL acetic acid, then 2.5 mL BAC water (3 mL total)
    • 1 mg or 2 mg once daily, 5 days a week
    • Dose at night, at least 90 minutes after the last meal
    • 12–16 week cycle, then 4–8 week washout

    Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

    Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

    • Catalog doses 1–2 mg, 5 days a week. The label is 1.28 mg (WR) or 1.4 mg (SV) daily; the trials used 2 mg daily.
    • Unit math: every unit value the catalog prints matches. Diluent totals: 10 mg in 3 mL, 20 mg in 5 mL.

    Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

    Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.

    Optimization & support

    Foundational support

    BProtect slow-wave sleep3
    Why
    The largest natural GH pulse comes in early slow-wave sleep. That's the physiology bedtime GH-secretagogue dosing tries to ride.
    Mechanism
    Slow-wave sleep and nocturnal GH release are tightly coupled. Both decline with age.
    Basis
    Inferred: GH physiology. Not tested as an add-on to this compound.
    Downside
    None.
    B150–300 min/wk moderate aerobic + strength work ≥2 days/wk4
    Why
    The proven fat-loss and metabolic-health lever, whatever compound is added.
    Mechanism
    Raises energy expenditure, improves insulin sensitivity and preserves lean mass.
    Basis
    General US guideline, not tested with this compound. Any 'exercise-mimetic' claim for a compound is no substitute.
    Downside
    None specific.

    Mechanistic support

    BDose away from carbohydrate / high-glucose meals5
    Why
    Raised glucose and insulin blunt GH release, which is why the catalog says to dose fasted.
    Mechanism
    Oral glucose suppresses GH secretion; acromegaly is diagnosed by the absence of that suppression.
    Basis
    Inferred: GH physiology. The catalog's specific fasting windows are untested.
    Downside
    Hypoglycemia risk if combined with insulin-lowering drugs while fasted.
    AMonitor fasting glucose / HbA1c2
    Why
    GH and IGF-1 raise insulin resistance.
    Mechanism
    GH counters insulin action. In tesamorelin trials HbA1c ≥6.5% developed in 5% vs 1% on placebo.
    Basis
    Demonstrated for tesamorelin (label); applied by analogy to other GH secretagogues.
    Downside
    None.

    Complementary interventions

    A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

    Redundancies & overlap

    Sermorelin, CJC-1295 (no DAC)Same GHRH receptor; alternatives, not add-ons.
    Ipamorelin, GHRP-2, GHRP-6Different receptor (ghrelin/GHS-R). Combined GHRH + GHS raises GH more than either alone in physiology studies, but no outcome trial tests the catalog blends.

    Specific cautions

    • AContraindicated: disrupted hypothalamic-pituitary axis, active malignancy, pregnancy.2
    • AElevated IGF-1: monitor it; effects of prolonged elevation unknown. Consider stopping if it stays high.2
    • AGlucose intolerance or diabetes may develop: check glucose before and during.2
    • AFluid retention: edema, joint pain, carpal tunnel.2

    Research gaps

    • Any outcome data outside HIV lipodystrophy.
    • The catalog's 5-days-a-week schedule and cycling are untested (the label is daily, continuous).
    • Stability of the catalog's acetic-acid/BAC mix.
