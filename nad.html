NAD+

Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide

Catalog: NAD: Buffered & Unbuffered · Cellular energy and antioxidants · Evidence ceiling C

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • CNAD+ carries electrons in energy metabolism and is consumed by sirtuins and PARPs (DNA repair).1
  • CIV NAD+ disappears from plasma almost completely for the first 2 hours of an infusion; it's rapidly taken up or broken down.1

Identity

ClassRedox coenzyme; substrate for sirtuins and PARPs
Regulatory statusNot an approved injectable drug; used compounded (IV infusions, SC injections).
Supplier formulation500 mg and 1,000 mg powder; 'buffered' and 'unbuffered' versions (catalog).

Intended effects

  • E'Anti-aging', energy, recovery (community and clinic use)2

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • CPilot PK study: 6-hour IV infusion (≈750 mg). No change in plasma NAD+ or its metabolites for the first 2 hours; urinary NAD+ and methylnicotinamide rose by 6 hours.1
    • CIV NAD+ commonly causes flushing, nausea, chest tightness and cramping at faster infusion rates (clinic and industry reports).1
    • N/ENo human study of SC NAD+ was found.1

    Preclinical findings

      Documented dosing & cycles

      E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)2

      • 500 mg + 2.5 mL BAC water (with benzyl alcohol), or 1,000 mg + 5 mL
      • Week 1: 25–50 mg 2×/week → weeks 3–6: 50–100 mg 4×/week → weeks 7–12: 50–100 mg 4–7×/week
      • Morning; a light meal may reduce side effects
      • Up to 12 weeks, then 4–6 week washout; warns against more than 3,000 mg/month

      Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

      Optimization & support

      Foundational support

      B150–300 min/wk moderate aerobic + strength work ≥2 days/wk3
      Why
      The proven fat-loss and metabolic-health lever, whatever compound is added.
      Mechanism
      Raises energy expenditure, improves insulin sensitivity and preserves lean mass.
      Basis
      General US guideline, not tested with this compound. Any 'exercise-mimetic' claim for a compound is no substitute.
      Downside
      None specific.

      Mechanistic support

      Complementary interventions

      BNicotinamide riboside (oral NAD+ precursor)4
      Why
      An oral, studied way to raise NAD+, unlike injected or nasal NAD+.
      Mechanism
      Converted to NMN, then NAD+ via the salvage pathway.
      Basis
      RCT (Martens 2018): well tolerated and raised NAD+ in middle-aged and older adults. Clinical benefits unproven.
      Downside
      Raising NAD+ isn't the same as a health benefit; long-term data limited.

      A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

      Redundancies & overlap

      Oral NAD precursors (NR, NMN)The same goal through a different route. Not on this injectable list.
      5-Amino-1MQProposed to raise NAD+ by blocking NNMT; preclinical.

      Specific cautions

      • CFlushing, nausea, chest tightness and cramping are common with IV NAD+, and rate-related.1
      • ECatalog storage note: use mixed NAD+ within 30–45 days, shorter than most peptides.2

      Research gaps

      • Any outcome benefit of injected NAD+.
      • SC pharmacokinetics.
      • What 'buffered vs unbuffered' means for the catalog product.
