Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • AThe approved use is IV methylene blue (ProvayBlue) for acquired methemoglobinemia (accelerated approval). Oral methylene blue isn't FDA-approved for anything.3
    • COral LMTM (a methylene-blue derivative) failed its Alzheimer's phase 3 primary endpoints.2
    • CNo adequate trials support cognitive or anti-aging use in healthy adults.2
