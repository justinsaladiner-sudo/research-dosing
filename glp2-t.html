03

Tirzepatide

Dual GIP / GLP-1 receptor agonist

Catalog: GLP2-T · Metabolic compounds · Evidence ceiling A

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • ASingle peptide agonizing GIPR and GLP-1R, with a C20 fatty-diacid moiety for albumin binding.1
  • AGLP-1R: appetite suppression, slower gastric emptying, glucose-dependent insulin secretion. GIPR: added insulinotropic and possibly adipose/tolerability effects; how much GIP adds in humans is still debated.1

Identity

AliasesZepbound, Mounjaro (approved products) · catalog name GLP2-T
ClassDual GIP / GLP-1 receptor agonist (acylated peptide)
TargetsGIP receptor · GLP-1 receptor
Half-life≈5 days, so once-weekly dosing
Regulatory statusFDA-approved: Mounjaro (T2D), Zepbound (chronic weight management; obstructive sleep apnea with obesity). The catalog powder is not these products.
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 10, 30 and 60 mg vials (catalog).

Intended effects

  • AChronic weight management1
  • AGlycemic control in type 2 diabetes1
  • AModerate-to-severe obstructive sleep apnea with obesity1

Secondary findings

  • BHead to head vs semaglutide (SURMOUNT-5, 72 wk): −20.2% vs −13.7%, and waist −18.4 cm vs −13.0 cm.3
  • BAdverse-event discontinuation in SURMOUNT-5: 6.1% tirzepatide vs 8.0% semaglutide (not powered for safety).3

Human evidence

  • ASURMOUNT-1 (n=2,539, no diabetes, 72 wk): −15.0% (5 mg), −19.5% (10 mg), −20.9% (15 mg) vs −3.1% placebo.2
  • BSURMOUNT-5 (n=751, max tolerated dose): beat semaglutide by ≈6.5 percentage points.3
  • CLimitation: like semaglutide, weight returns after stopping (class pattern; tirzepatide-specific withdrawal data not reviewed here).1

Preclinical findings

  • DRodent thyroid C-cell tumors: the basis of the class boxed warning. Human relevance unknown.1

Start at 2.5 mg (an initiation dose, not a maintenance dose). Go up 2.5 mg after at least 4 weeks per step. Maintenance is 5, 10 or 15 mg, with 15 mg the maximum.

E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)4

  • GLP-naive: start at 2.5 mg
  • Took any GLP for at least a month: start at 5 mg
  • Once per week, same day; any time of day; no fasting needed
  • Stay on each dose at least 4 weeks before increasing
  • No cycle documented

Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

  • Catalog claims 'average weight loss of 24.7% after 72 weeks'. No match in SURMOUNT-1 (−20.9% at 15 mg) or SURMOUNT-5 (−20.2%). Source unknown.
  • Catalog starts GLP-experienced users at 5 mg. The label always starts at 2.5 mg.
  • Unit math: 10 and 30 mg vials are 20 mg/mL and the 60 mg vial is 24 mg/mL. 7.5 mg = 37.5 units exactly; the catalog prints 36 (rounding, within tolerance).

Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.

Optimization & support

Foundational support

CProtein 1.2–1.6 g/kg/d during active loss5
Why
GLP-1-class drugs cut energy intake by 16–39%, so lean mass is at risk.
Mechanism
Supplies amino acids for muscle protein synthesis during an energy deficit. Base the target on adjusted body weight or fat-free mass, not actual weight. Avoid ≥2 g/kg/d long term.
Basis
Inferred: joint advisory for GLP-1 therapy. Not tested with this compound.
Downside
Protein above muscle needs may convert to fat. Protein/fat-heavy meals slow gastric emptying and can worsen constipation.
BResistance training ≥3×/wk + ≥150 min/wk aerobic5
Why
Protein alone is unlikely to preserve muscle without structured strength training.
Mechanism
Mechanical loading signals muscle to keep lean and bone mass during rapid weight loss. Aerobic work alone preserves less.
Basis
Inferred: weight-loss trials + GLP-1 retrospective data via the joint advisory.
Downside
None specific. Ramp up gradually if intake is very low.
CHydration + electrolytes5
Why
Nausea, vomiting and diarrhea are common with incretin drugs.
Mechanism
GI fluid loss leads to volume depletion. Postmarket GLP-1 reports link this to renal impairment around initiation and dose escalation.
Basis
Inferred: GLP-1 class postmarket reports via the advisory.
Downside
Watch renal function if renal impairment is present.
CMicronutrient sufficiency (consider vitamin D, calcium, B12, multivitamin-mineral)5
Why
At intakes under ≈1,200 kcal/d (women) or 1,800 kcal/d (men), deficiencies become likely.
Mechanism
Nutrients of concern: iron, calcium, magnesium, zinc, and vitamins A, D, E, K, B1, B12, C. Warning signs: unusual fatigue, hair loss, poor wound healing.
Basis
Inferred: GLP-1 class advisory.
Downside
Dose supplements to need. Fat-soluble vitamins can accumulate.

Mechanistic support

CFiber, magnesium citrate, PEG 3350 for constipation5
Why
Constipation is a common on-target effect of GLP-1 agonism.
Mechanism
Slower GI transit. Fiber adds bulk. Magnesium and PEG draw water into the stool osmotically.
Basis
Inferred: advisory management guidance for GLP-1 drugs.
Downside
Magnesium can cause diarrhea or build up when kidney function is reduced.
CSmall, low-fat meals; stop eating at satiety5
Why
Reduces nausea and diarrhea, which are worse with large or high-fat meals.
Mechanism
Delayed gastric emptying plus a large gastric load causes distension and nausea.
Basis
Inferred: advisory guidance.
Downside
Watch that protein targets are still met.

Complementary interventions

BCreatine monohydrate with resistance training6
Why
Helps protect lean mass while drug-driven weight loss strips muscle as well as fat.
Mechanism
Raises muscle creatine stores, supporting training volume and lean-mass gains.
Basis
Strong evidence for lean mass with resistance training (ISSN 2017). Not tested together with GLP-1 drugs; inferred.
Downside
Water weight gain (scale noise); caution with existing kidney disease.

A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

Redundancies & overlap

Semaglutide (GLP1-S), retatrutide (GLP3-R), mazdutide, survodutideSame incretin receptors. Treat as alternatives, not add-ons. The catalog's advice to rotate them between cycles has no trial basis.

Specific cautions

  • ABoxed warning: contraindicated with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2.1
  • AOral hormonal contraceptives: absorption is reduced. Use a non-oral method or add a barrier method for 4 weeks after starting and after each dose increase.1
  • APancreatitis, gallbladder disease, acute kidney injury from dehydration.1
  • AHypoglycemia when combined with insulin or sulfonylureas.1
  • ADelayed gastric emptying: aspiration risk under anesthesia.1

Research gaps

  • Identity, purity and sterility of research powder vs the approved pens/vials.
  • Catalog GLP-experienced start at 5 mg skips the label's 2.5 mg initiation step.
  • Rotating between incretin drugs (catalog advice) is untested.
