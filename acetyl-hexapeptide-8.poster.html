What is Acetyl Hexapeptide-8 (Argireline)?

Acetyl Hexapeptide-8 (Argireline): topical SNAP-25-mimicking "Botox-like" cosmetic peptide. Small studies: ~30% wrinkle-depth reduction in 10 women over 30 days (10% solution); later small trials, some unblinded. Whether it penetrates skin is doubtful; part of the effect may be hydration.
