What is MK-677 (Ibutamoren)?

MK-677 (Ibutamoren): oral ghrelin-receptor agonist (GH secretagogue). Nass 2008 (65 healthy adults aged 60–81, 25 mg daily, 12 months): GH and IGF-1 rose to young-adult levels; fat-free mass up ~1.1 kg vs −0.5 kg placebo; no strength gain; fasting glucose and insulin resistance rose.

Clinical evidence

  • BNass 2008 (65 healthy adults aged 60–81, 25 mg daily, 12 months): GH and IGF-1 rose to young-adult levels; fat-free mass up ~1.1 kg vs −0.5 kg placebo; no strength gain; fasting glucose and insulin resistance rose.
  • CHip-fracture trial stopped early with more congestive heart failure on ibutamoren (≈6.5% vs 1.7%; secondary sources).
  • CNot FDA-approved; banned in sport.
