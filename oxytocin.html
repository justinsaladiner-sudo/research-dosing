Oxytocin

Neurohypophyseal peptide hormone

Catalog: Oxytocin · Hormone and reproductive signaling · Evidence ceiling A

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • AContracts uterine smooth muscle and triggers milk let-down. Centrally it modulates social behavior and stress. It has an intrinsic antidiuretic (water-retaining) effect.1

Identity

AliasesPitocin (approved IV/IM product)
ClassNonapeptide hormone (posterior pituitary)
TargetsOxytocin receptor (uterus, breast, brain); some vasopressin-receptor activity
Regulatory statusApproved IV/IM for obstetric use (labor induction or augmentation; not elective induction). Subcutaneous daily use is not approved or established.
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 10 mg vial (catalog).

Intended effects

  • ALabor induction / augmentation (IV, hospital)1
  • BSocial, mood, sexual effects (community use; intranasal research)2

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • BSikich 2021 (NEJM, children and adolescents 3–17 with autism, intranasal, target 48 IU/day, 24 weeks): no benefit over placebo on social or cognitive measures.2
    • N/ENo peer-reviewed human study of daily subcutaneous oxytocin was found.2

    Preclinical findings

      Documented dosing & cycles

      E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)3

      • Mix 10 mg with 2.5 mL BAC water; use 30- or 50-unit syringes
      • Start at 100 mcg once daily; up to 200 mcg daily
      • 8-week cycle, then 4-week washout

      Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

      Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

      • Catalog: '6 units (200 mcg)'. At 4 mg/mL, 6 units is 240 mcg (20% over); 200 mcg is 5 units.
      • 100 mcg = 2.5 units; the catalog prints 3 (rounding).

      Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

      Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.

      Catalog math mismatches: 10 mg vial, 0.2 mg: catalog says 6 units, math says 5

      10 mg vial + 2.5 mL BAC water = 4 mg/mL

      Optimization & support

      Foundational support

      B150–300 min/wk moderate aerobic + strength work ≥2 days/wk4
      Why
      The proven fat-loss and metabolic-health lever, whatever compound is added.
      Mechanism
      Raises energy expenditure, improves insulin sensitivity and preserves lean mass.
      Basis
      General US guideline, not tested with this compound. Any 'exercise-mimetic' claim for a compound is no substitute.
      Downside
      None specific.

      Mechanistic support

      Complementary interventions

      A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

      Redundancies & overlap

      PT-141, kisspeptinOverlapping sexual-function marketing, different mechanisms.

      Specific cautions

      • AAntidiuretic effect: water intoxication is possible at high doses or with heavy fluid intake (label).1
      • AContracts the uterus: avoid in pregnancy outside obstetric care.1
      • AThe label notes inherent pressor (blood-pressure) properties at large doses.1

      Research gaps

      • Any human data for daily SC use.
      • Conversion between the catalog's mcg and the label's USP units (not verified here).
