What is hCG?

hCG is a hormone that acts like LH, the pituitary signal that tells the testes to make testosterone. It's FDA-approved for male hypogonadism and fertility treatment. It's widely used alongside testosterone therapy to keep the testes working. Its label explicitly says it doesn't help with weight loss.

Clinical evidence

  • ALabel, male hypogonadotropic hypogonadism: 500–1,000 USP units 3×/week for 3 weeks, then the same dose 2×/week for 3 weeks (one of several regimens).
  • BCoviello 2005 (29 healthy men on testosterone enanthate 200 mg/week): hCG 125, 250 or 500 IU every other day kept intratesticular testosterone up in a dose-related way

Common side effects

  • Headache, irritability, fatigue
  • Edema; gynecomastia
  • Injection-site pain
  • Women: ovarian hyperstimulation

Important considerations

  • Contraindicated: precocious puberty, prostate cancer or other androgen-dependent tumors, pregnancy
  • In women (ovulation induction): ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome, multiple births, arterial thromboembolism
  • Androgen-driven fluid retention: caution with heart or kidney disease, epilepsy, migraine, asthma
  • Adverse reactions: headache, irritability, depression, fatigue, edema, gynecomastia, injection-site pain

Key notes (catalog)

  • Low dose: 250–500 IU 3×/week (1,000 IU vial + 1 mL)
  • Standard: 500 IU 3 days/week for 12 weeks (5,000 IU vial + 2 mL)
  • High dose: 1,500 → 2,000 → 1,000 IU 3×/week over 12 weeks
  • Morning; 8–16 week protocol, then 8–16 week washout

Reconstitution example

Add 2 mL BAC water → 2500 IU/mL

  1. Swab both vial tops with an alcohol pad
  2. Draw the BAC water (3 mL syringe, 22–23G)
  3. Inject slowly into the peptide vial
  4. Roll gently between your palms; don't shake
  5. Let it sit 15–30 min until fully dissolved

Catalog: refrigerate reconstituted vials in an opaque, airtight container, not in the door. Use within 60–90 days of mixing (catalog claim, not stability data). Powder: room temperature 30–60 days, fridge up to 1 year.
