L-Carnitine

Fatty-acid transport cofactor (levocarnitine)

Catalog: L-Carnitine · Cellular energy and antioxidants · Evidence ceiling A

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • AShuttles long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane for beta-oxidation, and buffers excess acyl groups. Dialysis patients can run low.1

Identity

AliasesLevocarnitine (Carnitor and generics)
ClassAmino-acid derivative; carries long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria
Regulatory statusLevocarnitine injection is FDA-approved for carnitine deficiency (metabolic disorders, and end-stage kidney disease on dialysis).
Supplier formulationPre-made solution. The catalog doesn't state the vial size; '10 units = 50 mg' implies 500 mg/mL.

Intended effects

  • ACarnitine deficiency in end-stage kidney disease on dialysis1
  • EFat oxidation / endurance (community use)2

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • ALabel (dialysis): 10–20 mg/kg dry body weight as a slow IV bolus after each dialysis session. Metabolic disorders: 50 mg/kg.1
    • N/EFat-loss benefit from injection in people who aren't deficient wasn't found in the label or any source reviewed.1

    Preclinical findings

      Documented dosing & cycles

      A Label dosing (levocarnitine injection)1

      10–20 mg/kg IVAfter each dialysis

      IV in deficiency states. The catalog's SC use and fat-loss goal are outside the label.

      E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)2

      • 50 mg/day (weeks 1–2), then 100 mg (weeks 3–8), then 150–200 mg (weeks 9–12)
      • Timing depends on the goal; fasted for fat oxidation
      • 12-week cycle, then 6–12 week washout

      Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

      Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

      • Vial size isn't stated; 500 mg/mL is implied by the catalog's own unit math.
      • Unit math: every unit value the catalog prints matches that concentration.

      Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

      Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.

      Foundational support

      B150–300 min/wk moderate aerobic + strength work ≥2 days/wk3
      Why
      The proven fat-loss and metabolic-health lever, whatever compound is added.
      Mechanism
      Raises energy expenditure, improves insulin sensitivity and preserves lean mass.
      Basis
      General US guideline, not tested with this compound. Any 'exercise-mimetic' claim for a compound is no substitute.
      Downside
      None specific.

      Mechanistic support

      Complementary interventions

      A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

      Redundancies & overlap

      Lipotropic blends (LIPO-C etc.)Several catalog blends already contain L-carnitine.

      Specific cautions

      • ASeizures have been reported with oral or IV levocarnitine, with or without prior seizure history.1

      Research gaps

      • Fat-loss evidence for injected carnitine without deficiency.
      • SC tolerability and absorption.
