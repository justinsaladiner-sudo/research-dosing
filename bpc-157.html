BPC-157

Gastric pentadecapeptide ('body protection compound')

Catalog: BPC-157 · Repair and matrix peptides · Evidence ceiling C

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • DProposed: promotes angiogenesis and growth-factor signaling, which drives tissue repair. Based on animal research, mostly from one Croatian group.1

Identity

AliasesBody Protection Compound-157
ClassSynthetic 15-amino-acid peptide based on a sequence from a human gastric protein
Regulatory statusNot approved. Reported as an FDA Category 2 bulk substance (compounding restricted, safety concerns), per secondary sources. Banned in sport (WADA).
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 10 mg and 20 mg vials (catalog).

Intended effects

  • ETendon, ligament, muscle and gut repair (community use)2

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • CNo published human efficacy RCTs. Three small uncontrolled human pilots exist.1
    • CKnee pain (Lee & Padgett 2021): retrospective chart review of intra-articular injection, no controls; most patients reported relief (≈87–92% across reports).1
    • CInterstitial cystitis pilot (2024, 12 women, into the bladder): 10 of 12 reported complete resolution; no adverse events reported.1
    • CIV safety pilot (2025, 2 adults): well tolerated, with no changes in vitals, ECG or labs. Too small to establish safety.1

    Preclinical findings

    • DA large rodent literature reports faster healing of tendon, ligament, muscle, bone and GI lesions. Not independently replicated in humans.1

    Documented dosing & cycles

    E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)2

    • 250 mcg (week 1) → 500 mcg (weeks 2–4) → 750 mcg (weeks 5–8), 1–3 times a day
    • 5 days a week (2 rest days); 7 days OK for an 8-week cycle
    • 8–10 week cycle, then 4–6 week washout

    Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

    Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

    • 20 mg vial (8 mg/mL, 80 mcg/unit): '4 units = 250 mcg' is really 320 mcg (28% over); '10 units = 750 mcg' is really 800 mcg.
    • 10 mg vial matches (40 mcg/unit).

    Optimization & support

    Foundational support

    AVitamin C sufficiency (RDA 75 mg women / 90 mg men)3
    Why
    Collagen can't be made properly without vitamin C.
    Mechanism
    Cofactor for prolyl and lysyl hydroxylases that stabilize collagen.
    Basis
    Established nutrition science (NIH ODS). Extra beyond sufficiency isn't shown to boost repair-peptide effects.
    Downside
    Very high doses can cause GI upset and raise kidney-stone risk in susceptible people (NIH ODS).

    Mechanistic support

    BGelatin 15 g + vitamin C (~50 mg), 1 hour before training / rehab loading4
    Why
    Feeds collagen building blocks to tendons and ligaments right when loading stimulates repair.
    Mechanism
    Gelatin raises blood glycine, proline and hydroxyproline (peak ~1 h); vitamin C is the cofactor for collagen hydroxylation; brief loading drives connective-tissue uptake.
    Basis
    Demonstrated for a collagen-synthesis marker (≈ doubled) in 8 men (Shaw 2017). Not tested with any repair peptide; no injury-outcome data.
    Downside
    Small study; a marker, not healing. Gelatin adds ~60 kcal per 15 g.

    Complementary interventions

    A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

    Redundancies & overlap

    TB-500 (catalog 'Wolverine' stack)Also marketed for repair; animal data only; no combination study.

    Specific cautions

    • DPro-angiogenic mechanism: a theoretical concern with any existing tumor (inferred; untested in humans).1
    • CNo human safety data beyond tiny pilots.1
    • CProhibited in tested sport.1

    Research gaps

    • Any controlled human efficacy trial.
    • Long-term safety, and repeated daily SC dosing.
