LL-37

Human cathelicidin antimicrobial peptide

Catalog: LL-37 · Immune and other signaling peptides · Evidence ceiling B

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • BDisrupts bacterial membranes and biofilms. Also modulates inflammation and promotes re-epithelialization, the basis for testing it on chronic wounds.1

Identity

AliasesCathelicidin, hCAP18 C-terminal peptide
Class37-amino-acid cationic antimicrobial host-defense peptide (the only human cathelicidin)
TargetsMicrobial membranes; immune modulation; wound-healing signaling
Regulatory statusNot approved. Topical LL-37 reached phase 2 for venous leg ulcers.
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 5 mg vial (catalog).

Intended effects

  • BHealing hard-to-heal venous leg ulcers (topical)1
  • ESystemic immune / antimicrobial support (community use, injected)2

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • BPhase 1/2 (34 patients, venous leg ulcers, topical twice weekly × 4 weeks): healing rate about 6× placebo at 0.5 mg/mL (p=0.003) and 3× at 1.6 mg/mL (p=0.088). Ulcer area −68% and −50%. 3.2 mg/mL was no better than placebo. No safety concerns.1
    • CA later phase 2b (148 patients) reportedly missed significance overall (secondary source).1
    • N/ENo human data for injected LL-37.1

      E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)2

      • 5 mg vial + 2.5 mL BAC water
      • Weekly ladder 50 → 450 mcg once daily over 8 weeks
      • Daily for 4 weeks, then 5 days/week
      • 8–12 week cycle, then 4–8 week washout

      Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

      Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

      • Weeks 5–12: the catalog's units are short of its stated mcg. 300 mcg = 15 u (catalog 13), 350 = 17.5 (15), 400 = 20 (18), 450 = 22.5 (20). Following the units gives about 13% less than stated.
      • Typo: 'Week 4: 10 units (200cg)' means 200 mcg.

      Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

      Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.

      Catalog math mismatches: 5 mg vial, 0.3 mg: catalog says 13 units, math says 15; 5 mg vial, 0.35 mg: catalog says 15 units, math says 17.5; 5 mg vial, 0.4 mg: catalog says 18 units, math says 20; 5 mg vial, 0.45 mg: catalog says 20 units, math says 22.5
