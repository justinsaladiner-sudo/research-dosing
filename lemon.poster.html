What is Lemon Bottle?

Lemon Bottle is a cosmetic solution, mainly bromelain, lecithin and riboflavin, that's injected into fat with claims it dissolves it. No efficacy has been shown. Swiss and Finnish regulators warned against it, and Swiss lab tests found products that didn't match their labels. The approved option for under-chin fat is Kybella.

Common side effects

  • Severe bruising, swelling
  • Infections, abscesses
  • Necrosis (reported)

Important considerations

  • Infection, abscess and necrosis reports
  • Product contents may not match the label (Swiss testing)
  • Self-injecting large volumes (up to 100 mL per session) into fat carries infection and tissue-injury risk; no safety data exist

Key notes (catalog)

  • Map the area in a grid; inject 1 mL (thin areas), 1.5 mL (moderate) or 2 mL (dense fat) per point
  • 3–5 sessions, exactly 7 days apart
  • Limits: 10–20 mL per session for small areas; 30 mL per area; 100 mL per session overall
