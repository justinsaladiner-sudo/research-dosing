What is SLU-PP-332?

SLU-PP-332 is a synthetic small molecule that activates estrogen-related receptors. These switch on an endurance-exercise-like program in muscle. In obese mice it raised energy expenditure and cut fat mass without changing food intake. It has never been tested in people.

Key notes (catalog)

  • Mix 10 mg with 2 mL BAC water and 0.5 mL DMSO (catalog links a retail DMSO)
  • Weeks 1–4: 1.25 mg once daily; weeks 5–8: 2.5 mg once daily; 5–7 days/week
  • 8–16 week cycle, then 4–8 week washout
  • Morning to early afternoon; no fasting needed

Reconstitution example

Add 2.5 mL BAC water → 4 mg/mL

  1. Swab both vial tops with an alcohol pad
  2. Draw the BAC water (3 mL syringe, 22–23G)
  3. Inject slowly into the peptide vial
  4. Roll gently between your palms; don't shake
  5. Let it sit 15–30 min until fully dissolved

Catalog: refrigerate reconstituted vials in an opaque, airtight container, not in the door. Use within 60–90 days of mixing (catalog claim, not stability data). Powder: room temperature 30–60 days, fridge up to 1 year.
