Cartalax

Khavinson short-peptide bioregulator · cartilage (chondrocytes) and connective tissue

Catalog: Cartalax · Bioregulators · Evidence ceiling D

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • DClaimed to restore normal gene expression and protein synthesis in cartilage (chondrocytes) and connective tissue. The proposed mechanism (short peptides acting on DNA/chromatin) comes from the originating research network and hasn't been independently replicated.2

Identity

ClassShort synthetic peptide 'bioregulator' (Khavinson group, St Petersburg) · sequence Ala-Glu-Asp
Target tissue (claimed)cartilage (chondrocytes) and connective tissue
Regulatory statusNot approved in the US or EU; sold as a research peptide / supplement.
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 20 mg vial (catalog).

    Optimization & support

    Foundational support

    AVitamin C sufficiency (RDA 75 mg women / 90 mg men)3
    Why
    Collagen can't be made properly without vitamin C.
    Mechanism
    Cofactor for prolyl and lysyl hydroxylases that stabilize collagen.
    Basis
    Established nutrition science (NIH ODS). Extra beyond sufficiency isn't shown to boost repair-peptide effects.
    Downside
    Very high doses can cause GI upset and raise kidney-stone risk in susceptible people (NIH ODS).

    Mechanistic support

    BGelatin 15 g + vitamin C (~50 mg), 1 hour before training / rehab loading4
    Why
    Feeds collagen building blocks to tendons and ligaments right when loading stimulates repair.
    Mechanism
    Gelatin raises blood glycine, proline and hydroxyproline (peak ~1 h); vitamin C is the cofactor for collagen hydroxylation; brief loading drives connective-tissue uptake.
    Basis
    Demonstrated for a collagen-synthesis marker (≈ doubled) in 8 men (Shaw 2017). Not tested with any repair peptide; no injury-outcome data.
    Downside
    Small study; a marker, not healing. Gelatin adds ~60 kcal per 15 g.

    Complementary interventions

    A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

    Redundancies & overlap

    Other Khavinson bioregulators (Epitalon, Pinealon, the rest of this group)Same concept and research source; often stacked by tissue. No combination data.

    Specific cautions

    • N/ENo independent human safety data.2

    Research gaps

    • Independent (non-developer) human trials.
    • Verified sequence and identity of the catalog product.
