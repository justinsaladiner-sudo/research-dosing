What is Kisspeptin?

Kisspeptin is a natural brain peptide that switches on GnRH neurons, which makes the pituitary release LH and FSH. In IVF trials a single dose triggered egg maturation more safely than hCG. Its repeated use to raise testosterone is untested.

Clinical evidence

  • BPhase 2 (60 IVF patients at high risk of ovarian hyperstimulation), single kisspeptin-54 trigger at 4 doses: egg maturation in 95%
  • BThe rationale: hCG triggers are the main cause of ovarian hyperstimulation, and kisspeptin gives a shorter, more physiological LH surge.

Common side effects

  • Well tolerated in IVF research
  • Possible desensitization with frequent use (catalog)

Important considerations

  • Repeated or continuous dosing may desensitize the system (the catalog itself warns of this)
  • No safety data for repeated SC dosing outside research settings

Key notes (catalog)

  • 200 mcg once daily, every other day, fasted
  • 4-week cycle, then 4-week washout
  • Claims: '1 mcg/kg is best', 'fasting raises the LH response 5–6×', 'desensitization if used too often; 2×/week optimal' (unverified)

Reconstitution example

Add 1.5 mL BAC water → 3.3 mg/mL

  1. Swab both vial tops with an alcohol pad
  2. Draw the BAC water (3 mL syringe, 22–23G)
  3. Inject slowly into the peptide vial
  4. Roll gently between your palms; don't shake
  5. Let it sit 15–30 min until fully dissolved

Catalog: refrigerate reconstituted vials in an opaque, airtight container, not in the door. Use within 60–90 days of mixing (catalog claim, not stability data). Powder: room temperature 30–60 days, fridge up to 1 year.
