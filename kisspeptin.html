Kisspeptin

Hypothalamic GnRH-neuron activator

Catalog: Kisspeptin · Hormone and reproductive signaling · Evidence ceiling B

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • BKisspeptin is the upstream switch for GnRH release, so dosing it raises LH from the body's own pituitary rather than replacing LH like hCG does.1

Identity

AliasesKisspeptin-10, kisspeptin-54 (metastin). The catalog doesn't say which isoform it sells.
ClassEndogenous neuropeptide (KISS1 gene product)
TargetsKISS1R (GPR54) on GnRH neurons, which drives GnRH, then LH/FSH, then sex steroids
Regulatory statusInvestigational; not approved.
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 5 mg vial (catalog).

Intended effects

  • BTriggering egg maturation in IVF (kisspeptin-54)1
  • ERaising LH/testosterone (community use)2

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • BPhase 2 (60 IVF patients at high risk of ovarian hyperstimulation), single kisspeptin-54 trigger at 4 doses: egg maturation in 95%. Live birth rate per transfer 45%. Highest yield at 12.8 nmol/kg.1
    • BThe rationale: hCG triggers are the main cause of ovarian hyperstimulation, and kisspeptin gives a shorter, more physiological LH surge.1

    Preclinical findings

      Documented dosing & cycles

      B IVF trigger (Abbara 2015)1

      Single doseKisspeptin-54, weight-based (nmol/kg)

      A one-time trigger injection, not a repeated regimen. Not comparable to the catalog's every-other-day use.

      E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)2

      • 200 mcg once daily, every other day, fasted
      • 4-week cycle, then 4-week washout
      • Claims: '1 mcg/kg is best', 'fasting raises the LH response 5–6×', 'desensitization if used too often; 2×/week optimal' (unverified)

      Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.
