TB-500

Thymosin beta-4 fragment

Catalog: TB-500 · Repair and matrix peptides · Evidence ceiling D

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • DThymosin β4 binds actin and promotes cell migration, angiogenesis and repair. TB-500 is marketed as carrying the active region; most of the literature is on full-length Tβ4, not the fragment.1

Identity

AliasesThymosin β4 fragment (commonly described as Ac-LKKTETQ)
ClassSynthetic fragment of thymosin β4, the actin-binding region
Regulatory statusNot approved. Prohibited at all times in sport (WADA). Reported compounding restrictions are unverified.
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 10 mg vial (catalog).

Intended effects

  • ESoft-tissue injury repair (community use)2

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • N/ENo human trials of TB-500 itself.1
    • CFull-length thymosin β4 eye drops (RGN-259) had mixed phase 3 results in dry eye. Doesn't transfer to injected TB-500.1

    Preclinical findings

    • DAnimal and cell work on full-length Tβ4: wound, cardiac and corneal repair signals.1

    Documented dosing & cycles

    E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)2

    • 10 mg vial + 2 mL BAC water
    • Week 1: 1 mg twice a week (separate days)
    • Weeks 2–8: 1–2.5 mg twice a week; or 3–5 mg/week split over 2–3 days
    • 8-week cycle, then 4–8 week washout

    Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

    Optimization & support

    Foundational support

    AVitamin C sufficiency (RDA 75 mg women / 90 mg men)3
    Why
    Collagen can't be made properly without vitamin C.
    Mechanism
    Cofactor for prolyl and lysyl hydroxylases that stabilize collagen.
    Basis
    Established nutrition science (NIH ODS). Extra beyond sufficiency isn't shown to boost repair-peptide effects.
    Downside
    Very high doses can cause GI upset and raise kidney-stone risk in susceptible people (NIH ODS).

    Mechanistic support

    BGelatin 15 g + vitamin C (~50 mg), 1 hour before training / rehab loading4
    Why
    Feeds collagen building blocks to tendons and ligaments right when loading stimulates repair.
    Mechanism
    Gelatin raises blood glycine, proline and hydroxyproline (peak ~1 h); vitamin C is the cofactor for collagen hydroxylation; brief loading drives connective-tissue uptake.
    Basis
    Demonstrated for a collagen-synthesis marker (≈ doubled) in 8 men (Shaw 2017). Not tested with any repair peptide; no injury-outcome data.
    Downside
    Small study; a marker, not healing. Gelatin adds ~60 kcal per 15 g.

    Complementary interventions

    A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

    Redundancies & overlap

    BPC-157Catalog 'Wolverine' pairing; both lack human efficacy data.

    Specific cautions

    • DPro-angiogenic and pro-migration biology: a theoretical concern with existing tumors (inferred).1
    • CProhibited in tested sport.1
    • N/ENo human safety data.1

    Research gaps

    • Any human data for the fragment.
    • Exact identity: sources disagree (7-residue fragment vs '17-amino-acid').
