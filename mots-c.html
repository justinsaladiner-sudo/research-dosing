MOTS-c

Mitochondrial-derived peptide

Catalog: MOTS-C · Cellular energy and antioxidants · Evidence ceiling D

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • DMouse work: MOTS-c acts on skeletal muscle, improving insulin sensitivity and preventing diet-induced obesity, with exercise-like metabolic effects.1

Identity

Class16-amino-acid peptide encoded in mitochondrial DNA (12S rRNA region)
TargetsProposed: AMPK activation in skeletal muscle; metabolic stress signaling
Regulatory statusNot approved. An analog (CB4211) reached phase 1b.
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 10, 20 and 40 mg vials (catalog).

Intended effects

  • DMetabolic health / insulin sensitivity / exercise capacity (community use)1

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • N/ENo controlled human trials of MOTS-c itself were found.2
    • BIts analog CB4211, phase 1b (obese adults with fatty liver, 25 mg daily × 4 weeks, n=20): ALT −21% vs −4%, AST −28% vs −11%, glucose −6% vs 0%. No serious adverse events. Small and exploratory; a different molecule.2

    Preclinical findings

    • DLee 2015 (Cell Metab): prevented high-fat-diet obesity and insulin resistance in mice; reversed age-related muscle insulin resistance.1

    Documented dosing & cycles

    E Catalog protocols (researchdosing.com)3

    • 5 mg once every 5 days, or 3 mg once every 3 days, for 8–10 weeks
    • Morning on an empty stomach, or 30–60 min before fasted exercise
    • 8–10 week cycle, then 4–5 week washout
    • Catalog suggests 10–14 days of SS-31 first (no data)

    Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.
