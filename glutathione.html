Glutathione

Endogenous tripeptide antioxidant

Catalog: Glutathione · Cellular energy and antioxidants · Evidence ceiling B

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • BThe main intracellular antioxidant: it neutralizes reactive oxygen species and conjugates toxins for clearance. Cells mostly make their own from cysteine, glycine and glutamate.1

Identity

AliasesGSH, reduced glutathione (γ-glutamyl-cysteinyl-glycine)
ClassEndogenous antioxidant tripeptide
Regulatory statusNot an FDA-approved injectable drug in the US; used compounded.
Supplier formulation1,500 mg powder; mixed with 7 mL BAC water (catalog).

Intended effects

  • EAntioxidant / detox support (community use)2
  • BParkinson's disease symptoms (studied IV)1

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • BHauser 2009 pilot RCT (21 Parkinson's patients, IV 1,400 mg 3×/week for 4 weeks): well tolerated; a 2.8-point UPDRS improvement vs placebo, not significant (p=0.32).1
    • BA later meta-analysis: glutathione may mildly improve motor scores in Parkinson's without more adverse events.1
    • N/ENo human study of SC glutathione was found. Bioavailability of injected glutathione wasn't established this wave.1

      Complementary interventions

      BGlycine + N-acetylcysteine (GlyNAC)3
      Why
      Supplies the rate-limiting building blocks the body uses to make its own glutathione, by mouth.
      Mechanism
      Cysteine (from NAC) and glycine are glutathione precursors.
      Basis
      A 16-week RCT in older adults raised glutathione; a larger 2-week dose trial didn't overall (only in people starting low). Mixed. Trial doses were weight-based.
      Downside
      NAC: GI upset; interacts with nitroglycerin (headache/hypotension).

      A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

      Redundancies & overlap

      NAD+ (catalog stack)Different pathway (redox cofactor); no combination data.
      Lipo-C / lipotropicsSome catalog blends add antioxidants; overlap in purpose only.

      Specific cautions

      • EThe catalog's maximum-cycle and washout rules have no basis in the studies.2
      • ECompounded or research glutathione: sterility and endotoxin aren't documented.2

      Research gaps

      • SC pharmacokinetics.
      • Any benefit outside Parkinson's at the doses studied.
