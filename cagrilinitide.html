04

Cagrilintide

Long-acting amylin analog

Catalog: Cagrilinitide · Metabolic compounds · Evidence ceiling B

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • BAmylin is co-secreted with insulin. Its receptors in the area postrema and hypothalamus drive satiety and slow gastric emptying. This pathway is separate from GLP-1R, which is why it's paired with semaglutide.1

Identity

AliasesCagrilintide (INN; the catalog spells it 'Cagrilinitide'), component of CagriSema
ClassLong-acting acylated amylin analog (non-selective amylin/calcitonin receptor agonist)
TargetsAmylin receptors (calcitonin receptor + RAMP) · calcitonin receptor
Regulatory statusInvestigational. Not approved alone or as CagriSema at capture.
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 10 mg vial (catalog).

Intended effects

  • BWeight management, alone or with semaglutide (CagriSema)1

Secondary findings

  • BCombined with semaglutide, weight loss exceeded either drug alone (REDEFINE 1).2

Human evidence

  • BPhase 2 (Lau 2021, 26 wk, 0.3–4.5 mg weekly): −6.0% to −10.8% vs −3.0% placebo. 4.5 mg beat liraglutide 3.0 mg (−10.8% vs −9.0%). Weight was still falling at week 26.1
  • BREDEFINE 1 (n=3,417, 68 wk): CagriSema 2.4/2.4 mg −20.4% vs −3.0% placebo (treatment policy); −22.7% in adherent participants. Escalation took 16 weeks.2
  • BREDEFINE 2 (T2D): CagriSema −13.7% vs −3.4% placebo at 68 wk.2
  • BGI adverse events in phase 2: 41–63% vs 32% placebo, mainly nausea (20–47% vs 18%).1

    E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)4

    • Weekly steps: 0.2 → 0.4 → 0.75 → 1.2 → 1.7 → 2.2 mg (weeks 1–6), then 2.7 mg (weeks 7–8)
    • Same day each week; stay hydrated
    • 8-week cycle, then 4–8 week washout
    • Don't exceed 12 consecutive weeks

    Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

    Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

    • Escalation: the catalog steps up every week to 2.7 mg in 7 weeks. REDEFINE 1 took 16 weeks to reach 2.4 mg, and phase 2 took up to 6 weeks.
    • Top dose: the catalog's 2.7 mg is above the 2.4 mg phase 3 dose.
    • Catalog: '23–24.4% at 68 weeks combined with semaglutide'. REDEFINE 1 reports −20.4% (treatment policy) or −22.7% (adherent).
    • Name: the INN is cagrilintide.
    • Unit math: every unit value the catalog prints matches (5 mg/mL).

    Optimization & support

    Foundational support

    CProtein 1.2–1.6 g/kg/d during active loss5
    Why
    GLP-1-class drugs cut energy intake by 16–39%, so lean mass is at risk.
    Mechanism
    Supplies amino acids for muscle protein synthesis during an energy deficit. Base the target on adjusted body weight or fat-free mass, not actual weight. Avoid ≥2 g/kg/d long term.
    Basis
    Inferred: joint advisory for GLP-1 therapy. Not tested with this compound.
    Downside
    Protein above muscle needs may convert to fat. Protein/fat-heavy meals slow gastric emptying and can worsen constipation.
    BResistance training ≥3×/wk + ≥150 min/wk aerobic5
    Why
    Protein alone is unlikely to preserve muscle without structured strength training.
    Mechanism
    Mechanical loading signals muscle to keep lean and bone mass during rapid weight loss. Aerobic work alone preserves less.
    Basis
    Inferred: weight-loss trials + GLP-1 retrospective data via the joint advisory.
    Downside
    None specific. Ramp up gradually if intake is very low.
    CHydration + electrolytes5
    Why
    Nausea, vomiting and diarrhea are common with incretin drugs.
    Mechanism
    GI fluid loss leads to volume depletion. Postmarket GLP-1 reports link this to renal impairment around initiation and dose escalation.
    Basis
    Inferred: GLP-1 class postmarket reports via the advisory.
    Downside
    Watch renal function if renal impairment is present.

    Mechanistic support

    CSmall, low-fat meals; stop eating at satiety5
    Why
    Reduces nausea and diarrhea, which are worse with large or high-fat meals.
    Mechanism
    Delayed gastric emptying plus a large gastric load causes distension and nausea.
    Basis
    Inferred: advisory guidance.
    Downside
    Watch that protein targets are still met.
    CFiber, magnesium citrate, PEG 3350 for constipation5
    Why
    Constipation is a common on-target effect of GLP-1 agonism.
    Mechanism
    Slower GI transit. Fiber adds bulk. Magnesium and PEG draw water into the stool osmotically.
    Basis
    Inferred: advisory management guidance for GLP-1 drugs.
    Downside
    Magnesium can cause diarrhea or build up when kidney function is reduced.

    Complementary interventions

    BSemaglutide (CagriSema pairing)2
    Why
    The only combination with phase 3 data.
    Mechanism
    GLP-1R plus amylin receptor: two satiety pathways.
    Basis
    Demonstrated: REDEFINE 1 (−20.4% vs −3.0%), more than either drug alone.
    Downside
    Additive GI effects. The trial escalated both drugs together over 16 weeks.
    BCreatine monohydrate with resistance training6
    Why
    Helps protect lean mass while drug-driven weight loss strips muscle as well as fat.
    Mechanism
    Raises muscle creatine stores, supporting training volume and lean-mass gains.
    Basis
    Strong evidence for lean mass with resistance training (ISSN 2017). Not tested together with GLP-1 drugs; inferred.
    Downside
    Water weight gain (scale noise); caution with existing kidney disease.

    A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

    Redundancies & overlap

    Pramlintide (Symlin)Same amylin pathway; short-acting, mealtime, approved for insulin users.
    GLP-1 / GIP agonistsNot redundant: different receptors. The combination with semaglutide is studied; with tirzepatide or retatrutide it isn't.

    Specific cautions

    • BGI effects are dose-related. The catalog's weekly escalation is much faster than the trials'.1
    • AWith insulin: the amylin analog pramlintide carries a boxed warning for severe insulin-induced hypoglycemia (class analogy).3
    • ASlowed gastric emptying: aspiration risk under anesthesia (class analogy).3

    Research gaps

    • No approved product or label.
    • Monotherapy beyond 26 weeks.
    • The catalog's 8-week cycle and weekly escalation have no trial basis.
    • Combinations with tirzepatide or retatrutide (the catalog sells a GLP3-R/cagrilintide blend).
