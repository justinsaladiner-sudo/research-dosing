What is Semaglutide?

Semaglutide is a once-weekly GLP-1 receptor agonist: the molecule in Wegovy and Ozempic. It reduces appetite and slows gastric emptying, and it improves blood-sugar control. Of the drugs on this list, it has the deepest evidence base, including a cardiovascular outcomes trial.

Clinical evidence

  • ASTEP 1 (n=1,961, no diabetes, 68 wk, 2.4 mg): −14.9% vs −2.4% placebo. 50.5% lost ≥15% and 32.0% lost ≥20% (vs 4.9% / 1.7%).
  • BSTEP UP (n=1,407, 72 wk): 7.2 mg ≈ −20.7% (efficacy estimand, ≈19% treatment-regimen). 31.2% reached ≥25% loss vs 15.3% on 2.4 mg.
  • BSURMOUNT-5 (head to head, 72 wk): tirzepatide −20.2% vs semaglutide −13.7%.
  • BLimitation: efficacy depends on staying on drug; most of the loss returns after stopping.

Common side effects

  • Nausea, diarrhea, vomiting and constipation are the most common (dose-related)
  • Mostly during dose escalation
  • Dysesthesia more frequent at 7.2 mg (22%)
  • Gallbladder events with rapid weight loss

Important considerations

  • Boxed warning: contraindicated with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2
  • Pancreatitis, gallbladder disease (cholelithiasis), acute kidney injury from dehydration
  • Hypoglycemia when combined with insulin or sulfonylureas
  • Diabetic retinopathy complications reported in T2D with rapid glucose lowering
  • Delayed gastric emptying: affects oral drug absorption and aspiration risk under anesthesia
  • Pregnancy: stop at least 2 months before a planned pregnancy (long half-life)

Key notes (catalog)

  • Doses listed from 0.25 mg up to 2.5 mg per week
  • Once weekly, same day each week
  • Stay on each dose at least 4 weeks before increasing
  • Take it before bed if prone to side effects
  • No cycle documented

Reconstitution example

Add 1 mL BAC water → 5 mg/mL

  1. Swab both vial tops with an alcohol pad
  2. Draw the BAC water (3 mL syringe, 22–23G)
  3. Inject slowly into the peptide vial
  4. Roll gently between your palms; don't shake
  5. Let it sit 15–30 min until fully dissolved

Catalog: refrigerate reconstituted vials in an opaque, airtight container, not in the door. Use within 60–90 days of mixing (catalog claim, not stability data). Powder: room temperature 30–60 days, fridge up to 1 year.

Common combinations

  • Amylin agonist (cagrilintide) B
  • Creatine monohydrate with resistance training B
  • Protein 1.2–1.6 g/kg/d during active loss C
  • Resistance training ≥3×/wk + ≥150 min/wk aerobic B

Avoid / use caution with

  • Personal or family history of medullary thyroid cancer or MEN 2
  • Insulin or sulfonylureas (hypoglycemia)
  • Other GLP-1 / GIP agonists (stacked GI effects)
  • History of pancreatitis
  • Planned pregnancy: stop at least 2 months before
  • Upcoming anesthesia (aspiration risk)

Similar options

  • Tirzepatide (GLP2-T), retatrutide (GLP3-R), mazdutide, survodutide: All agonize GLP-1R
  • Cagrilintide: Not redundant (amylin receptor)

Research gaps

  • Identity, purity and sterility of research powder vs the approved product; no data on the reconstituted powder's stability.
  • Catalog's 2.5 mg top dose matches no approved dose (2.0, 2.4 or 7.2 mg).
  • Long-term outcome of weight cycling on/off drug.
  • Lean-mass share of weight lost on drug.
