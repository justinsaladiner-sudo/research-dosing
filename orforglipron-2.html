Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • AFDA-approved April 1, 2026 as Foundayo for chronic weight management: the first GLP-1 pill for weight loss, taken any time without food or water restrictions.2
    • BATTAIN phase 3 (>3,000 adults, 72 weeks). About 12% average loss at the top dose is reported; dose-specific figures weren't verified.2
    • ABoxed warning: thyroid C-cell tumors; contraindicated with personal/family history of MTC or MEN 2.2

    Preclinical findings

      Documented dosing & cycles

      E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)1

      • Week 1: ¼ tablet (1.5 mg); week 2: ½ (3 mg); weeks 3–6: 1 (6 mg); weeks 7–10: 2 (12 mg); once daily
      • Catalog links Amazon pill splitters
      • 18–36 week cycle, then 18–36 week washout

      Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

      Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

      • Splitting tablets into quarters for 1.5 mg doses isn't accurate; the approved product comes in fixed-strength tablets.
      • The catalog cycles it (18–36 weeks on/off); the approved drug is continuous chronic therapy.

      Optimization & support

      Foundational support

      CProtein 1.2–1.6 g/kg/d during active loss3
      Why
      GLP-1-class drugs cut energy intake by 16–39%, so lean mass is at risk.
      Mechanism
      Supplies amino acids for muscle protein synthesis during an energy deficit. Base the target on adjusted body weight or fat-free mass, not actual weight. Avoid ≥2 g/kg/d long term.
      Basis
      Inferred: joint advisory for GLP-1 therapy. Not tested with this compound.
      Downside
      Protein above muscle needs may convert to fat. Protein/fat-heavy meals slow gastric emptying and can worsen constipation.
      BResistance training ≥3×/wk + ≥150 min/wk aerobic3
      Why
      Protein alone is unlikely to preserve muscle without structured strength training.
      Mechanism
      Mechanical loading signals muscle to keep lean and bone mass during rapid weight loss. Aerobic work alone preserves less.
      Basis
      Inferred: weight-loss trials + GLP-1 retrospective data via the joint advisory.
      Downside
      None specific. Ramp up gradually if intake is very low.
      CHydration + electrolytes3
      Why
      Nausea, vomiting and diarrhea are common with incretin drugs.
      Mechanism
      GI fluid loss leads to volume depletion. Postmarket GLP-1 reports link this to renal impairment around initiation and dose escalation.
      Basis
      Inferred: GLP-1 class postmarket reports via the advisory.
      Downside
      Watch renal function if renal impairment is present.

      Mechanistic support

      Complementary interventions

      BCreatine monohydrate with resistance training4
      Why
      Helps protect lean mass while drug-driven weight loss strips muscle as well as fat.
      Mechanism
      Raises muscle creatine stores, supporting training volume and lean-mass gains.
      Basis
      Strong evidence for lean mass with resistance training (ISSN 2017). Not tested together with GLP-1 drugs; inferred.
      Downside
      Water weight gain (scale noise); caution with existing kidney disease.

      A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.
