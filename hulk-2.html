    Preclinical findings

      Documented dosing & cycles

      E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)1

      • 20 mg (10 + 10) + 2.5 mL BAC water
      • 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 units (stated 100 → 250 mcg) daily over 12 weeks; optional 5 units weeks 12–16
      • Inject slowly (flushing, pins-and-needles)
      • 12–16 week cycle, then 8–16 week washout

      Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

      Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

      • The dose table is internally inconsistent: 1 → 4 units can't equal 100 → 250 mcg. At 8 mg/mL combined (80 mcg/unit), 4 units is 320 mcg combined (stated 250) and 5 units is 400 (stated 300). The catalog admits it rounds partial units.
      • The stated mcg don't say whether they mean per peptide or combined; this sheet uses combined, which fits the first rows best.

      Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

      Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.

      Catalog math mismatches: 20 mg vial, 0.25 mg: catalog says 4 units, math says 3.1; 20 mg vial, 0.3 mg: catalog says 5 units, math says 3.8
