What is LL-37?

LL-37 is the only human cathelicidin, an antimicrobial peptide that also helps wounds heal. Applied to chronic leg ulcers, the lower doses sped healing several-fold in a small trial. Injected LL-37 has never been studied in humans.

Clinical evidence

  • BPhase 1/2 (34 patients, venous leg ulcers, topical twice weekly × 4 weeks): healing rate about 6× placebo at 0.5 mg/mL (p=0.003) and 3× at 1.6 mg/mL (p=0.088)
  • CA later phase 2b (148 patients) reportedly missed significance overall (secondary source).
  • N/ENo human data for injected LL-37.

Common side effects

  • Topical: no safety concerns in the wound trial
  • Injected: no data
  • Injection-site irritation (cationic peptide)

Important considerations

  • In the wound trial the highest dose (3.2 mg/mL) was no better than placebo: more isn't better
  • No safety data for injection

Reconstitution example

Add 2.5 mL BAC water → 2 mg/mL

  1. Swab both vial tops with an alcohol pad
  2. Draw the BAC water (3 mL syringe, 22–23G)
  3. Inject slowly into the peptide vial
  4. Roll gently between your palms; don't shake
  5. Let it sit 15–30 min until fully dissolved

Catalog: refrigerate reconstituted vials in an opaque, airtight container, not in the door. Use within 60–90 days of mixing (catalog claim, not stability data). Powder: room temperature 30–60 days, fridge up to 1 year.
