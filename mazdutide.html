05

Mazdutide

Dual GLP-1 / glucagon receptor agonist

Catalog: Mazdutide · Metabolic compounds · Evidence ceiling A

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • BGLP-1R: appetite suppression, slower gastric emptying, insulin secretion. GCGR: proposed rise in energy expenditure and hepatic fat oxidation (the oxyntomodulin concept).1

Identity

AliasesIBI362 (Innovent) / LY3305677 (Lilly) · brand Xinermei (China)
ClassOxyntomodulin-analog dual agonist (peptide)
TargetsGLP-1 receptor · Glucagon receptor
Regulatory statusApproved in China (NMPA, June 2025) for chronic weight management; under NMPA review for T2D. No FDA or EMA approval.
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 10 mg vial (catalog).

Intended effects

  • AChronic weight management1
  • BGlycemic control in type 2 diabetes (under review in China)2

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • BGLORY-1 (n=610 Chinese adults, 48 wk): −12.05% (4 mg), −14.84% (6 mg) vs −0.47% placebo. At 32 wk: −10.97% / −13.38%. Published in NEJM 2025; figures here are company-reported.1
    • AApproval rested mainly on GLORY-1. Trials so far are in Chinese populations.2

      Optimization & support

      Foundational support

      CProtein 1.2–1.6 g/kg/d during active loss5
      Why
      GLP-1-class drugs cut energy intake by 16–39%, so lean mass is at risk.
      Mechanism
      Supplies amino acids for muscle protein synthesis during an energy deficit. Base the target on adjusted body weight or fat-free mass, not actual weight. Avoid ≥2 g/kg/d long term.
      Basis
      Inferred: joint advisory for GLP-1 therapy. Not tested with this compound.
      Downside
      Protein above muscle needs may convert to fat. Protein/fat-heavy meals slow gastric emptying and can worsen constipation.
      BResistance training ≥3×/wk + ≥150 min/wk aerobic5
      Why
      Protein alone is unlikely to preserve muscle without structured strength training.
      Mechanism
      Mechanical loading signals muscle to keep lean and bone mass during rapid weight loss. Aerobic work alone preserves less.
      Basis
      Inferred: weight-loss trials + GLP-1 retrospective data via the joint advisory.
      Downside
      None specific. Ramp up gradually if intake is very low.
      CHydration + electrolytes5
      Why
      Nausea, vomiting and diarrhea are common with incretin drugs.
      Mechanism
      GI fluid loss leads to volume depletion. Postmarket GLP-1 reports link this to renal impairment around initiation and dose escalation.
      Basis
      Inferred: GLP-1 class postmarket reports via the advisory.
      Downside
      Watch renal function if renal impairment is present.
      CMicronutrient sufficiency (consider vitamin D, calcium, B12, multivitamin-mineral)5
      Why
      At intakes under ≈1,200 kcal/d (women) or 1,800 kcal/d (men), deficiencies become likely.
      Mechanism
      Nutrients of concern: iron, calcium, magnesium, zinc, and vitamins A, D, E, K, B1, B12, C. Warning signs: unusual fatigue, hair loss, poor wound healing.
      Basis
      Inferred: GLP-1 class advisory.
      Downside
      Dose supplements to need. Fat-soluble vitamins can accumulate.

      Mechanistic support

      CFiber, magnesium citrate, PEG 3350 for constipation5
      Why
      Constipation is a common on-target effect of GLP-1 agonism.
      Mechanism
      Slower GI transit. Fiber adds bulk. Magnesium and PEG draw water into the stool osmotically.
      Basis
      Inferred: advisory management guidance for GLP-1 drugs.
      Downside
      Magnesium can cause diarrhea or build up when kidney function is reduced.
      CSmall, low-fat meals; stop eating at satiety5
      Why
      Reduces nausea and diarrhea, which are worse with large or high-fat meals.
      Mechanism
      Delayed gastric emptying plus a large gastric load causes distension and nausea.
      Basis
      Inferred: advisory guidance.
      Downside
      Watch that protein targets are still met.

      Complementary interventions

      BCreatine monohydrate with resistance training6
      Why
      Helps protect lean mass while drug-driven weight loss strips muscle as well as fat.
      Mechanism
      Raises muscle creatine stores, supporting training volume and lean-mass gains.
      Basis
      Strong evidence for lean mass with resistance training (ISSN 2017). Not tested together with GLP-1 drugs; inferred.
      Downside
      Water weight gain (scale noise); caution with existing kidney disease.

      A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

      Redundancies & overlap

      SurvodutideThe same GLP-1/glucagon dual mechanism; a direct alternative.
      Retatrutide (GLP3-R)Adds GIP on top of the same two receptors.
      Semaglutide, tirzepatideOverlap at GLP-1R. The catalog's advice to rotate between cycles is untested.

      Specific cautions

      • AGLP-1 class warnings apply: MTC/MEN 2, pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, hypoglycemia with insulin/sulfonylureas.4
      • CGlucagon-receptor agonism raised heart rate with retatrutide. Mazdutide's own heart-rate data weren't reviewed this wave.4

      Research gaps

      • Escalation schedule and adverse-event rates from the GLORY-1 paper (not pulled this wave).
      • Data outside Chinese populations.
      • Long-term cardiovascular and heart-rate effects.
      • Research powder vs the approved Chinese product.
