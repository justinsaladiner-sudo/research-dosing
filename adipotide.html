Adipotide

Fat-vasculature-targeted proapoptotic peptide

Catalog: Adipotide · Metabolic compounds · Evidence ceiling D

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • DHomes to prohibitin on white-fat blood vessels and triggers apoptosis of those vessel cells. Fat tissue loses its blood supply and is reabsorbed.1

Identity

AliasesFTPP (fat-targeted proapoptotic peptide), prohibitin-TP01
ClassPeptidomimetic: a fat-vessel homing motif fused to a proapoptotic D(KLAKLAK)2 sequence
TargetsProhibitin on the blood vessels that feed white fat
Regulatory statusExperimental; no published human data.
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 10 mg vial (catalog).

Intended effects

  • DObesity (fat-mass reduction)1

Secondary findings

  • DObese monkeys also showed better insulin resistance markers.2

Human evidence

  • N/ENo published human data.2

Preclinical findings

  • DObese rhesus monkeys (n=10 treated, 5 controls), daily SC injection for 28 days: ≈11% body-weight loss; abdominal fat −27%.1
  • DKidney toxicity was the main side effect: dose-dependent, predictable and reversible.2

Documented dosing & cycles

E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)3

  • Mix 10 mg with 2.5 mL BAC water
  • Days 1–7: 0.33 mg; days 9–14: 0.55 mg; days 15–28: 1 mg, once daily
  • 28-day cycle, then 12–28 day washout
  • No fasting needed

Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

  • The catalog schedule skips day 8 (days 1–7, then 9–14).
  • 0.55 mg = 13.75 units exactly; the catalog prints 14 (rounding).
  • Unit math otherwise matches (4 mg/mL).

Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.

Optimization & support

Foundational support

Why
The proven fat-loss and metabolic-health lever, whatever compound is added.
Mechanism
Raises energy expenditure, improves insulin sensitivity and preserves lean mass.
Basis
General US guideline, not tested with this compound. Any 'exercise-mimetic' claim for a compound is no substitute.
Downside
None specific.

Mechanistic support

Complementary interventions

A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

Redundancies & overlap

None in the catalogNo other catalog compound works by killing fat-vessel cells.

Specific cautions

  • DKidney toxicity in primates (dose-dependent). The main reason for concern.2
  • DA proapoptotic payload: any off-target homing damages healthy tissue.1
  • N/ENo human safety data.2

Research gaps

  • Any human data.
  • Whether the kidney effects reverse fully after repeat cycles.
  • Why development stalled (claims that kidney toxicity ended it are unverified).
