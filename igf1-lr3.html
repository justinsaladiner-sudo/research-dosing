IGF-1 LR3

Long-acting IGF-1 analog

Catalog: IGF-1 LR3 · Growth hormone axis · Evidence ceiling D

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • DIGF-1 receptor agonist with reduced IGF-binding-protein capture, so more of it stays free and active for longer than native IGF-1. Insulin-like glucose lowering is inherent to IGF-1 signaling.1

Identity

AliasesLong R3 IGF-1
ClassIGF-1 analog: Arg3 substitution plus a 13-amino-acid N-terminal extension, giving weak binding to IGF-binding proteins
TargetsIGF-1 receptor (and the insulin receptor at high levels)
Regulatory statusNot approved; a cell-culture reagent. The approved IGF-1 drug is mecasermin (Increlex), a different molecule.
Supplier formulation1 mg vial, reconstituted in 0.6% acetic acid (catalog).

Intended effects

  • EMuscle growth / recovery (community use)2

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • N/ENo human studies of IGF-1 LR3.1
    • AIts approved relative, mecasermin, is used for severe primary IGF-1 deficiency in children. Its main risk is severe hypoglycemia, including seizures.1

    Preclinical findings

      Documented dosing & cycles

      E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)2

      • Mix 1 mg with 2.5 mL 0.6% acetic acid
      • 33 → 50 → 75 → 100 mcg once daily over 8 weeks
      • Bedtime or fasted
      • 8-week cycle, then 4–8 week washout

      Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

      Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

      • Catalog advises fasted dosing. The approved IGF-1 drug's label says to dose with a meal to avoid hypoglycemia.
      • Unit math: every unit value the catalog prints matches (0.4 mg/mL = 4 mcg/unit).

      Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

      Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.

      Optimization & support

      Foundational support

      B150–300 min/wk moderate aerobic + strength work ≥2 days/wk3
      Why
      The proven fat-loss and metabolic-health lever, whatever compound is added.
      Mechanism
      Raises energy expenditure, improves insulin sensitivity and preserves lean mass.
      Basis
      General US guideline, not tested with this compound. Any 'exercise-mimetic' claim for a compound is no substitute.
      Downside
      None specific.

      Mechanistic support

      AEat around the dose; know hypoglycemia symptoms1
      Why
      IGF-1 lowers glucose the way insulin does.
      Mechanism
      IGF-1 receptor and insulin-receptor cross-activation increases glucose uptake.
      Basis
      Demonstrated for mecasermin (label: give it shortly before or after a meal; severe hypoglycemia occurred); applied by analogy.
      Downside
      This directly contradicts the catalog's 'take it fasted' advice.

      Complementary interventions

      A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

      Redundancies & overlap

      GH secretagogues (CJC, ipamorelin, GHRPs, sermorelin, tesamorelin)They raise IGF-1 indirectly. LR3 bypasses GH and the feedback loop entirely.

      Specific cautions

      • AHypoglycemia, possibly severe (mecasermin analogy). The catalog's fasted dosing makes it worse.1
      • AMecasermin is contraindicated with malignancy. IGF-1 signaling promotes cell growth.1
      • N/ENo human safety data for LR3 at any dose.1

      Research gaps

      • Any human data.
      • Pharmacokinetics of the extended-half-life analog in humans.
