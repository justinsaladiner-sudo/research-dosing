Bronchogen

Khavinson short-peptide bioregulator · bronchopulmonary tissue (bronchi, lungs)

Catalog: Bronchogen · Bioregulators · Evidence ceiling D

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • DClaimed to restore normal gene expression and protein synthesis in bronchopulmonary tissue (bronchi, lungs). The proposed mechanism (short peptides acting on DNA/chromatin) comes from the originating research network and hasn't been independently replicated.2

    Documented dosing & cycles

    E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)1

    • Mix with 3 mL BAC water
    • Days 1–7: 500 mcg/day; days 8–40: 2 mg/day
    • 6 days on, 1 day off each week
    • Bedtime, 2 h after the last meal
    • 40-day cycle; may repeat after 3–6 months

    Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

    Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

    • Vial size isn't stated; the unit math implies 20 mg in 3 mL.
    • 500 mcg = 7.5 units exactly; the catalog prints 8 (rounding).

    Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

    Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.
