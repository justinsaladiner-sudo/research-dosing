FOXO4-DRI

Senolytic peptide (FOXO4–p53 disruptor)

Catalog: Fox04-DRI · Immune and other signaling peptides · Evidence ceiling D

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • DBlocks FOXO4 from holding p53 in the nucleus of senescent cells. Released p53 then triggers apoptosis, selectively clearing senescent cells.1

Identity

AliasesProxofim
ClassD-retro-inverso peptide that mimics part of FOXO4
TargetsThe FOXO4–p53 interaction in senescent cells
Regulatory statusNo human trials; not approved.
Supplier formulationResearch powder. The catalog doesn't state the vial size; its unit math implies 10 mg in 3 mL. The catalog page is a stub (no description).

Intended effects

  • DClearing senescent cells (anti-aging)1

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • N/ENo human clinical trial has been completed.1

    Preclinical findings

    • DBaar 2017 (Cell): killed senescent cells selectively in culture. In fast-aging and naturally aged mice it restored fitness, fur density and kidney function.1

    Documented dosing & cycles

    E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)2

    • Mix with 3 mL BAC water
    • 250 mcg daily (weeks 1–4) → 375 mcg (weeks 5–8) → 500 mcg (weeks 9–16)
    • Daily, any time; 8–16 week cycle, then 4–8 week washout

    Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

    Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

    • Vial size isn't stated; 10 mg is implied by the unit math.
    • 250 mcg = 7.5 units exactly; the catalog prints 8 (rounding). Other values match.

    Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

    Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.
