What is Tirzepatide?

Tirzepatide is a once-weekly dual GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist: the molecule in Zepbound and Mounjaro. It's approved for weight management, type 2 diabetes and obesity-related sleep apnea, and it beat semaglutide head to head.

Clinical evidence

  • ASURMOUNT-1 (n=2,539, no diabetes, 72 wk): −15.0% (5 mg), −19.5% (10 mg), −20.9% (15 mg) vs −3.1% placebo.
  • BSURMOUNT-5 (n=751, max tolerated dose): beat semaglutide by ≈6.5 percentage points.
  • CLimitation: like semaglutide, weight returns after stopping (class pattern; tirzepatide-specific withdrawal data not reviewed here).

Common side effects

  • Nausea, diarrhea, vomiting, constipation: most common, dose-related
  • Mostly during dose escalation
  • Injection-site reactions
  • Gallbladder events with rapid weight loss

Important considerations

  • Boxed warning: contraindicated with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2
  • Oral hormonal contraceptives: absorption is reduced
  • Pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, acute kidney injury from dehydration
  • Hypoglycemia when combined with insulin or sulfonylureas
  • Delayed gastric emptying: aspiration risk under anesthesia

Key notes (catalog)

  • GLP-naive: start at 2.5 mg
  • Took any GLP for at least a month: start at 5 mg
  • Once per week, same day; any time of day; no fasting needed
  • Stay on each dose at least 4 weeks before increasing
  • No cycle documented

Reconstitution example

Add 1.5 mL BAC water → 20 mg/mL

  1. Swab both vial tops with an alcohol pad
  2. Draw the BAC water (3 mL syringe, 22–23G)
  3. Inject slowly into the peptide vial
  4. Roll gently between your palms; don't shake
  5. Let it sit 15–30 min until fully dissolved

Catalog: refrigerate reconstituted vials in an opaque, airtight container, not in the door. Use within 60–90 days of mixing (catalog claim, not stability data). Powder: room temperature 30–60 days, fridge up to 1 year.

Common combinations

  • Creatine monohydrate with resistance training B
  • Protein 1.2–1.6 g/kg/d during active loss C
  • Resistance training ≥3×/wk + ≥150 min/wk aerobic B

Avoid / use caution with

  • Personal or family history of medullary thyroid cancer or MEN 2
  • Oral contraceptives alone: add a backup method for 4 weeks after starting and after each dose increase
  • Insulin or sulfonylureas (hypoglycemia)
  • Other GLP-1 / GIP agonists (stacked GI effects)
  • History of pancreatitis
  • Upcoming anesthesia (aspiration risk)

Similar options

  • Semaglutide (GLP1-S), retatrutide (GLP3-R), mazdutide, survodutide: Same incretin receptors

Research gaps

  • Identity, purity and sterility of research powder vs the approved pens/vials.
  • Catalog GLP-experienced start at 5 mg skips the label's 2.5 mg initiation step.
  • Rotating between incretin drugs (catalog advice) is untested.
