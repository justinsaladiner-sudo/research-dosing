AOD-9604

Growth hormone C-terminal fragment (176–191) analog

Catalog: AOD-9604 · Metabolic compounds · Evidence ceiling B

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • BDesigned to keep the fat-metabolism effect attributed to hGH's C-terminus while avoiding its growth and glucose effects. No effect on IGF-1 in human trials.1

Identity

AliasesTyr-hGH(177–191), 'anti-obesity drug 9604' (Metabolic Pharmaceuticals)
ClassModified hGH C-terminal fragment (16-amino-acid peptide)
TargetsProposed lipolytic action without GH-receptor / IGF-1 effects
Regulatory statusNot approved. Australia's TGA rejected an obesity indication after the phase 2b failure. Reported as an FDA Category 2 bulk substance (compounding restricted) as of April 2026; secondary source.
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder. The catalog doesn't state the vial size; its unit math implies 5 mg in 2.5 mL (2 mg/mL).

Intended effects

  • BObesity / fat loss (failed in phase 2b)2

Secondary findings

  • BNo negative effect on glucose tolerance and no anti-drug antibodies across six randomized trials.1

Human evidence

  • BPhase 2b (24 wk, n=536): no significant weight loss vs placebo; reported around 2007. Secondary sources; the primary publication wasn't located.2
  • BSafety summary of six randomized placebo-controlled trials: well tolerated, no IGF-1 change, no glucose-tolerance effect.1
  • BPhase 2a (2002): more fat breakdown for several hours after a dose; ≈0.2 kg weight difference after one week.2

Preclinical findings

  • DRodent studies drove the original lipolysis claims. They weren't re-reviewed this wave; the human phase 2b result supersedes them for weight loss.2

Documented dosing & cycles

E Catalog protocols (researchdosing.com)3

  • Standard: 300 mcg once daily, 20 days on / 10 days off, repeat
  • Fat loss: 250 mcg twice daily (on waking, and ≥1.5 h after dinner), 90 days on / 30 off
  • Dose fasted; stay fasted 3–4 h after if possible
  • Mix with 2.5 mL BAC water

Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

  • Vial size isn't stated. 300 mcg = 15 units in 2.5 mL implies a 5 mg vial (2 mg/mL), and both protocols match that.
  • 250 mcg = 12.5 units exactly; the catalog prints 13 (rounding).
  • Catalog storage note: AOD-9604 is good for 30–45 days after mixing, not the usual 60–90.

Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.

Optimization & support

Foundational support

B150–300 min/wk moderate aerobic + strength work ≥2 days/wk4
Why
The proven fat-loss and metabolic-health lever, whatever compound is added.
Mechanism
Raises energy expenditure, improves insulin sensitivity and preserves lean mass.
Basis
General US guideline, not tested with this compound. Any 'exercise-mimetic' claim for a compound is no substitute.
Downside
None specific.
CProtein 1.2–1.6 g/kg/d during active weight reduction5
Why
Protects lean mass during an energy deficit.
Mechanism
Supplies amino acids for muscle protein synthesis. Base the target on adjusted body weight or fat-free mass. Avoid ≥2 g/kg/d long term.
Basis
Inferred: weight-reduction targets cited in the 2025 joint advisory. Not tested with this compound.
Downside
Excess protein beyond muscle needs isn't stored as muscle.

Mechanistic support

Complementary interventions

A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

Redundancies & overlap

HGH fragment 176–191 productsThe same fragment concept, usually sold under a different name.
Catalog stacks with GLP-1 drugsNo combination data. AOD-9604 adds no proven weight loss of its own.

Specific cautions

  • BLittle safety data beyond 24 weeks.1
  • EThe catalog's twice-daily protocol doubles exposure versus the studied regimens (which weren't re-verified dose by dose this wave).3

Research gaps

  • The primary phase 2b publication.
  • Any weight-loss benefit in humans (the best trial was negative).
  • Research-powder identity and purity. The catalog doesn't even state the vial size.
