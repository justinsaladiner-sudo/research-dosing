Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • BIntranasal bremelanotide WAS tested: the FDA halted the phase II nasal trials in 2007 because of blood-pressure increases, and the nasal form was abandoned in 2008. The developer blamed erratic nasal uptake causing high blood levels in some users; it was reformulated as the SC injection now approved.3

    Preclinical findings

      Documented dosing & cycles

      E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)1

      • 3–4 sprays (300–400 mcg) 30–60 min before sex
      • Catalog: "trials used 10–20 mg without adverse effects… we are not recommending a maximum"

      Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

      Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

      • DANGEROUS CLAIM: the catalog says nasal trials used 10–20 mg "without adverse effects" and sets no maximum. In fact the nasal trials were halted by the FDA over blood-pressure rises. The approved injection is 1.75 mg, at most once in 24 hours and 8 times a month.
