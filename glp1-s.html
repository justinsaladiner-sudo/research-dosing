02

Semaglutide

GLP-1 receptor agonist

Catalog: GLP1-S · Metabolic compounds · Evidence ceiling A

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • ASelective GLP-1R agonist. A C18 fatty-diacid side chain binds albumin, and the Aib8 substitution blocks DPP-4, giving the ≈1-week half-life.1
  • ACentral appetite suppression (hypothalamus/hindbrain), slower gastric emptying, glucose-dependent insulin secretion and glucagon suppression.1

Identity

AliasesWegovy, Ozempic (approved products) · catalog name GLP1-S
ClassLong-acting GLP-1 receptor agonist (acylated peptide)
TargetsGLP-1 receptor
Half-life≈1 week, so once-weekly dosing
Regulatory statusFDA-approved: Wegovy 2.4 mg (obesity, CV risk reduction), Ozempic up to 2 mg (T2D), Wegovy HD 7.2 mg (accelerated approval, March 2026). The catalog powder is not these products.
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 5 mg and 10 mg vials (catalog).

Intended effects

  • AChronic weight management (obesity, overweight with comorbidity)1
  • AGlycemic control in type 2 diabetes1
  • ACardiovascular risk reduction in established CVD with overweight/obesity4

Secondary findings

  • ASELECT (17,604 adults with CVD + overweight/obesity, no diabetes): major adverse cardiovascular events −20% (HR 0.80) on 2.4 mg.4
  • BWeight regain after stopping: STEP 1 participants regained about two-thirds of the lost weight within a year off drug.3
  • BDysesthesia (altered skin sensation): 22% on 7.2 mg vs 6% on 2.4 mg and 0.3% placebo (STEP UP).5

Human evidence

  • ASTEP 1 (n=1,961, no diabetes, 68 wk, 2.4 mg): −14.9% vs −2.4% placebo. 50.5% lost ≥15% and 32.0% lost ≥20% (vs 4.9% / 1.7%).2
  • BSTEP UP (n=1,407, 72 wk): 7.2 mg ≈ −20.7% (efficacy estimand, ≈19% treatment-regimen). 31.2% reached ≥25% loss vs 15.3% on 2.4 mg.5
  • BSURMOUNT-5 (head to head, 72 wk): tirzepatide −20.2% vs semaglutide −13.7%.6
  • BLimitation: efficacy depends on staying on drug; most of the loss returns after stopping.3

Preclinical findings

  • DRodent C-cell tumors (thyroid) at clinically relevant exposures are the basis of the class boxed warning. Human relevance unknown.1

E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)8

  • Doses listed from 0.25 mg up to 2.5 mg per week
  • Once weekly, same day each week
  • Stay on each dose at least 4 weeks before increasing
  • Take it before bed if prone to side effects
  • No cycle documented

Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

  • Catalog: 'half lost 17%, a third lost 20%'. STEP 1 says 50.5% lost ≥15% and 32.0% lost ≥20%.
  • The catalog lists doses to 2.5 mg; approved maintenance doses are 2.4 mg (Wegovy), up to 2 mg (Ozempic) or 7.2 mg (Wegovy HD).
  • Unit math: every unit value the catalog prints matches the computed value (both vials are 5 mg/mL).

Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.

Optimization & support

Foundational support

CProtein 1.2–1.6 g/kg/d during active loss9
Why
GLP-1-class drugs cut energy intake by 16–39%, so lean mass is at risk.
Mechanism
Supplies amino acids for muscle protein synthesis during an energy deficit. Base the target on adjusted body weight or fat-free mass, not actual weight. Avoid ≥2 g/kg/d long term.
Basis
Inferred: joint advisory for GLP-1 therapy. Not tested with this compound.
Downside
Protein above muscle needs may convert to fat. Protein/fat-heavy meals slow gastric emptying and can worsen constipation.
BResistance training ≥3×/wk + ≥150 min/wk aerobic9
Why
Protein alone is unlikely to preserve muscle without structured strength training.
Mechanism
Mechanical loading signals muscle to keep lean and bone mass during rapid weight loss. Aerobic work alone preserves less.
Basis
Inferred: weight-loss trials + GLP-1 retrospective data via the joint advisory.
Downside
None specific. Ramp up gradually if intake is very low.
CHydration + electrolytes9
Why
Nausea, vomiting and diarrhea are common with incretin drugs.
Mechanism
GI fluid loss leads to volume depletion. Postmarket GLP-1 reports link this to renal impairment around initiation and dose escalation.
Basis
Inferred: GLP-1 class postmarket reports via the advisory.
Downside
Watch renal function if renal impairment is present.
CMicronutrient sufficiency (consider vitamin D, calcium, B12, multivitamin-mineral)9
Why
At intakes under ≈1,200 kcal/d (women) or 1,800 kcal/d (men), deficiencies become likely.
Mechanism
Nutrients of concern: iron, calcium, magnesium, zinc, and vitamins A, D, E, K, B1, B12, C. Warning signs: unusual fatigue, hair loss, poor wound healing.
Basis
Inferred: GLP-1 class advisory.
Downside
Dose supplements to need. Fat-soluble vitamins can accumulate.

Mechanistic support

CFiber, magnesium citrate, PEG 3350 for constipation9
Why
Constipation is a common on-target effect of GLP-1 agonism.
Mechanism
Slower GI transit. Fiber adds bulk. Magnesium and PEG draw water into the stool osmotically.
Basis
Inferred: advisory management guidance for GLP-1 drugs.
Downside
Magnesium can cause diarrhea or build up when kidney function is reduced.
CSmall, low-fat meals; stop eating at satiety9
Why
Reduces nausea and diarrhea, which are worse with large or high-fat meals.
Mechanism
Delayed gastric emptying plus a large gastric load causes distension and nausea.
Basis
Inferred: advisory guidance.
Downside
Watch that protein targets are still met.

Complementary interventions

BAmylin agonist (cagrilintide)7
Why
Separate satiety pathway. The catalog sells a GLP1-S/cagrilintide blend.
Mechanism
Amylin receptor signaling in the area postrema/hypothalamus, which complements GLP-1R.
Basis
Demonstrated: CagriSema 2.4/2.4 mg, REDEFINE 1: −20.4% vs −3.0% placebo at 68 wk, more than either drug alone.
Downside
More GI effects than either alone. Trial used a 16-week escalation to 2.4 mg each.
BCreatine monohydrate with resistance training10
Why
Helps protect lean mass while drug-driven weight loss strips muscle as well as fat.
Mechanism
Raises muscle creatine stores, supporting training volume and lean-mass gains.
Basis
Strong evidence for lean mass with resistance training (ISSN 2017). Not tested together with GLP-1 drugs; inferred.
Downside
Water weight gain (scale noise); caution with existing kidney disease.

A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

Redundancies & overlap

Tirzepatide (GLP2-T), retatrutide (GLP3-R), mazdutide, survodutideAll agonize GLP-1R. Stacking adds GI burden with no evidence of added benefit; use them as alternatives.
CagrilintideNot redundant (amylin receptor). Combination studied: see complementary.

Specific cautions

  • ABoxed warning: contraindicated with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2.1
  • APancreatitis, gallbladder disease (cholelithiasis), acute kidney injury from dehydration.1
  • AHypoglycemia when combined with insulin or sulfonylureas.1
  • ADiabetic retinopathy complications reported in T2D with rapid glucose lowering.1
  • ADelayed gastric emptying: affects oral drug absorption and aspiration risk under anesthesia.1
  • APregnancy: stop at least 2 months before a planned pregnancy (long half-life).1

Research gaps

  • Identity, purity and sterility of research powder vs the approved product; no data on the reconstituted powder's stability.
  • Catalog's 2.5 mg top dose matches no approved dose (2.0, 2.4 or 7.2 mg).
  • Long-term outcome of weight cycling on/off drug.
  • Lean-mass share of weight lost on drug.
