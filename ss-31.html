SS-31 (Elamipretide)

Mitochondria-targeted tetrapeptide

Catalog: SS-31 · Cellular energy and antioxidants · Evidence ceiling A

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • ABinds cardiolipin and stabilizes the inner mitochondrial membrane and its electron-transport machinery. Barth syndrome is a cardiolipin disease, which is why it was the approval target.1

Identity

AliasesElamipretide, Bendavia, MTP-131 · brand Forzinity
ClassSzeto-Schiller tetrapeptide that concentrates in the inner mitochondrial membrane
TargetsCardiolipin (inner mitochondrial membrane)
Regulatory statusFDA accelerated approval Sept 19, 2025 (Forzinity) to improve muscle strength in Barth syndrome, patients ≥30 kg. Confirmatory trial required.
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 10, 30 and 50 mg vials (catalog).

Intended effects

  • AMuscle strength in Barth syndrome1
  • E'Mitochondrial optimization' (community use)2

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • ATAZPOWER (randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled): gains in knee-extensor strength, accepted as reasonably likely to predict benefit.1
    • AMost common side effects: mild-to-moderate injection-site reactions.1
    • AApproved dose: 40 mg SC once daily (≥30 kg; per label summary).1
    • AThe FDA first issued a Complete Response Letter (May 2025) before the September approval.1

    Preclinical findings

      Documented dosing & cycles

      A Label dosing (Forzinity)1

      40 mg SCOnce daily

      For Barth syndrome, patients ≥30 kg. The catalog's 1–6 mg doses are far below it and aimed at a different use.

      E Catalog protocols (researchdosing.com)2

      • 10 mg vial: 1 mg daily for 10–14 days before a MOTS-c cycle
      • Low: 1 → 2 mg 5 days/wk, then 3 → 4 mg 3 days/wk over 12 weeks
      • Standard: 5 mg 3–7 days/wk for 8–12 weeks; Max: up to 6 mg
      • Fasted; 8–12 week cycle, then 4–8 week washout

      Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

      Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

      • 30 mg vial: 4 mg = 26.7 units; the catalog prints 28 (borderline rounding). Every other value matches.
      • The approved dose is 40 mg daily; the catalog's standard is 5 mg.

      Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

      Optimization & support

      Foundational support

      B150–300 min/wk moderate aerobic + strength work ≥2 days/wk3
      Why
      The proven fat-loss and metabolic-health lever, whatever compound is added.
      Mechanism
      Raises energy expenditure, improves insulin sensitivity and preserves lean mass.
      Basis
      General US guideline, not tested with this compound. Any 'exercise-mimetic' claim for a compound is no substitute.
      Downside
      None specific.

      Mechanistic support

      Complementary interventions

      A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

      Redundancies & overlap

      MOTS-c, NAD+ (catalog stacks)Different mitochondrial angles; no combination data.

      Specific cautions

      • AInjection-site reactions are the main side effect in trials.1
      • AIts benefit outside Barth syndrome (healthy adults, aging) isn't established.1

      Research gaps

      • Effects at the catalog's low doses (1–6 mg).
      • Any benefit in people without a mitochondrial disease.
