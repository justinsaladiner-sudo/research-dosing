Read first

  • Your amounts are shown as recorded (historical). Recommendations change only what evidence or upper limits require; everything else stays your design.
  • No supplement here has been tested together with a peptide. Pairings are mechanistic or design choices, graded by the supplement's own evidence.
  • Cycling: nutrients don't generally need cycling. Your zinc/copper rotation is kept as designed (your labels, not validated effects). Time-box the experimental items (berberine, resveratrol, shilajit) and reassess; that's a precaution, not evidence.
  • NAD3, Cortisooth and OmegaXL are brand products whose formulas weren't verified, so they aren't graded.
