What is L-Carnitine?

L-carnitine carries fatty acids into mitochondria to be burned for energy. Injectable levocarnitine is FDA-approved to treat carnitine deficiency, such as in dialysis patients. Its use as a fat-loss shot has no supporting trials in this review.
