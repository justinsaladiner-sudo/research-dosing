CJC-1295 (no DAC)

Modified GRF(1–29)

Catalog: CJC No DAC · Growth hormone axis · Evidence ceiling E

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • ESame receptor as sermorelin; the amino-acid substitutions slow breakdown, so it gives a short GH pulse (half-life roughly 30 min per the catalog; not verified).3

Identity

AliasesModified GRF 1–29, 'mod GRF' (no-DAC form). Distinct from CJC-1295 with DAC.
ClassTetra-substituted GHRH(1–29) analog, DPP-4 resistant, without the albumin-binding DAC
TargetsPituitary GHRH receptor
Regulatory statusNever approved. The DAC form reached phase 2 before its trial was halted in 2006.
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 10 mg vial (catalog).

Intended effects

  • EGH/IGF-1 elevation for body composition and recovery (community use)3

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • N/ENo human trials of the no-DAC form were found.1
    • BThe DAC form (a different molecule): single doses in healthy adults raised GH and IGF-1 for 9–11 days; half-life 5.8–8.1 days; no serious adverse reactions.1
    • CThe DAC form's phase 2 in HIV visceral obesity (192 enrolled) was halted in July 2006 after a participant died. The attending physician attributed the death to coronary artery disease; a link to the drug was never established either way.2

    Preclinical findings

      Documented dosing & cycles

      E Catalog protocols (researchdosing.com)3

      • Once daily: 100 mcg (weeks 1–4), then 150 mcg (weeks 5–12)
      • Twice daily: 100 → 200 → 300 mcg; three times daily up to 500 mcg
      • Fasted 2–3 hours after eating; usually stacked with ipamorelin
      • 12-week cycle, then 6–12 week washout

      Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

      Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

      • Catalog: '5 units (150 mcg)'. At 4 mg/mL (40 mcg/unit), 5 units is 200 mcg, so it's 33% over the stated dose. The catalog's own 3×/day table correctly prints 150 mcg as 4 units.
      • 100 mcg = 2.5 units; the catalog prints 3 (rounding).

      Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

      Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.

      Optimization & support

      Foundational support

      BProtect slow-wave sleep5
      Why
      The largest natural GH pulse comes in early slow-wave sleep. That's the physiology bedtime GH-secretagogue dosing tries to ride.
      Mechanism
      Slow-wave sleep and nocturnal GH release are tightly coupled. Both decline with age.
      Basis
      Inferred: GH physiology. Not tested as an add-on to this compound.
      Downside
      None.
      B150–300 min/wk moderate aerobic + strength work ≥2 days/wk6
      Why
      The proven fat-loss and metabolic-health lever, whatever compound is added.
      Mechanism
      Raises energy expenditure, improves insulin sensitivity and preserves lean mass.
      Basis
      General US guideline, not tested with this compound. Any 'exercise-mimetic' claim for a compound is no substitute.
      Downside
      None specific.

      Mechanistic support

      BDose away from carbohydrate / high-glucose meals7
      Why
      Raised glucose and insulin blunt GH release, which is why the catalog says to dose fasted.
      Mechanism
      Oral glucose suppresses GH secretion; acromegaly is diagnosed by the absence of that suppression.
      Basis
      Inferred: GH physiology. The catalog's specific fasting windows are untested.
      Downside
      Hypoglycemia risk if combined with insulin-lowering drugs while fasted.
      AMonitor fasting glucose / HbA1c4
      Why
      GH and IGF-1 raise insulin resistance.
      Mechanism
      GH counters insulin action. In tesamorelin trials HbA1c ≥6.5% developed in 5% vs 1% on placebo.
      Basis
      Demonstrated for tesamorelin (label); applied by analogy to other GH secretagogues.
      Downside
      None.

      Complementary interventions

      A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

      Redundancies & overlap

      Sermorelin, tesamorelinSame GHRH receptor.
      Ipamorelin (catalog stack)Different receptor (GHS-R). Combined GHRH + GHS raises GH more than either in physiology studies; no outcome trial tests the stack.

      Specific cautions

      • N/ENo human safety data for this molecule.1
      • ARaised GH/IGF-1: glucose intolerance, fluid retention (class analogy).4
      • BDon't confuse with CJC-1295 DAC: it lasts days, not minutes, so dosing isn't interchangeable.1

      Research gaps

      • Any human data for the no-DAC form.
      • The half-life the catalog states.
