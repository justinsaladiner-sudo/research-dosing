What is Survodutide?

Survodutide is a once-weekly dual GLP-1 and glucagon receptor agonist from Boehringer Ingelheim and Zealand. It reached about 16.6% weight loss in phase 3, and it's being developed for fatty liver disease (MASH) as well as obesity.

Clinical evidence

  • BPhase 2 (n=386, no diabetes, 46 wk, planned treatment): −6.2% (0.6 mg), −12.5% (2.4 mg), −13.2% (3.6 mg), −14.9% (4.8 mg) vs −2.8% placebo. 18.7% at 4.8 mg is the actual-treatment analysis.
  • BPhase 2: adverse-event discontinuation 25% (pooled survodutide, mostly GI) vs 4% placebo
  • BSYNCHRONIZE-1 (no diabetes, 76 wk, 3.6/6.0 mg): up to −16.6% vs −3.2% placebo (efficacy estimand)
  • BSYNCHRONIZE-2 (T2D, 76 wk): up to −13.1% vs −3.1%; HbA1c down up to 1.21%.

Common side effects

  • Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation (dose-related)
  • Phase 2: 25% stopped for adverse events (mostly GI)
  • Heart-rate rise possible (glucagon class)

Important considerations

  • GI intolerance led to 25% discontinuation in phase 2
  • GLP-1 class warnings apply: MTC/MEN 2, pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, hypoglycemia with insulin/sulfonylureas
  • Glucagon-receptor agonism is associated with heart-rate increases in this drug class

Key notes (catalog)

  • Start at 1.6 mg; stay at least 4 weeks before increasing
  • Raise in 0.8 mg steps; maximum 6.4 mg
  • Once weekly, same day each week
  • 4.8 mg flagged as 'the clinical trial dose for 19% weight loss'

Reconstitution example

Add 1.5 mL BAC water → 6.7 mg/mL

  1. Swab both vial tops with an alcohol pad
  2. Draw the BAC water (3 mL syringe, 22–23G)
  3. Inject slowly into the peptide vial
  4. Roll gently between your palms; don't shake
  5. Let it sit 15–30 min until fully dissolved

Catalog: refrigerate reconstituted vials in an opaque, airtight container, not in the door. Use within 60–90 days of mixing (catalog claim, not stability data). Powder: room temperature 30–60 days, fridge up to 1 year.

Common combinations

  • Creatine monohydrate with resistance training B
  • Protein 1.2–1.6 g/kg/d during active loss C
  • Resistance training ≥3×/wk + ≥150 min/wk aerobic B

Avoid / use caution with

  • Fast escalation (GI intolerance)
  • Personal or family history of medullary thyroid cancer or MEN 2 (class)
  • Insulin or sulfonylureas (hypoglycemia)
  • Other incretin agonists

Similar options

  • Mazdutide: Same GLP-1/glucagon dual mechanism
  • Retatrutide (GLP3-R): Same two receptors plus GIP
  • Semaglutide, tirzepatide: Overlap at GLP-1R
  • Catalog stacks (glutathione + NAD+, AOD-9604, CJC/ipamorelin): No combination data for any of them

Research gaps

  • Dose-specific SYNCHRONIZE results (3.6 vs 6.0 mg) and the escalation schedule.
  • Cardiovascular outcomes.
  • Doses above 6.0 mg (the catalog goes to 6.4).
