09

SLU-PP-332

Estrogen-related receptor (ERR) agonist · 'exercise mimetic'

Catalog: SLU-PP-332 · Metabolic compounds · Evidence ceiling D

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • DERR activation switches on a skeletal-muscle program that resembles endurance training: more mitochondria and more fatty-acid oxidation.1

Identity

ClassSynthetic small-molecule pan-ERR agonist (ERRα/β/γ). Not a peptide.
TargetsEstrogen-related receptors, which regulate mitochondrial and oxidative-metabolism genes
Regulatory statusResearch chemical; no human trials.
Supplier formulation10 mg powder. The catalog mixes it with 2 mL BAC water plus 0.5 mL DMSO.

Intended effects

  • DMetabolic syndrome / obesity ('exercise mimetic')1

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • N/ENo human studies.1

    Preclinical findings

    • DDiet-induced obese and ob/ob mice: higher energy expenditure and fatty-acid oxidation, less fat mass, better glucose control. Food intake and voluntary activity didn't change.1
    • DPress coverage reports 12% body-weight loss in obese mice dosed twice daily for a month. Not confirmed in the primary paper.2

    Documented dosing & cycles

    E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)4

    • Mix 10 mg with 2 mL BAC water and 0.5 mL DMSO (catalog links a retail DMSO)
    • Weeks 1–4: 1.25 mg once daily; weeks 5–8: 2.5 mg once daily; 5–7 days/week
    • 8–16 week cycle, then 4–8 week washout
    • Morning to early afternoon; no fasting needed

    Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

    Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

    • The diluent volume includes 0.5 mL DMSO: total 2.5 mL, so 4 mg/mL. The catalog's units match that.
    • The catalog links a retail (Amazon) DMSO for an injectable mix.

    Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

    Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.

    Optimization & support

    Foundational support

    B150–300 min/wk moderate aerobic + strength work ≥2 days/wk5
    Why
    The proven fat-loss and metabolic-health lever, whatever compound is added.
    Mechanism
    Raises energy expenditure, improves insulin sensitivity and preserves lean mass.
    Basis
    General US guideline, not tested with this compound. Any 'exercise-mimetic' claim for a compound is no substitute.
    Downside
    None specific.
    CProtein 1.2–1.6 g/kg/d during active weight reduction6
    Why
    Protects lean mass during an energy deficit.
    Mechanism
    Supplies amino acids for muscle protein synthesis. Base the target on adjusted body weight or fat-free mass. Avoid ≥2 g/kg/d long term.
    Basis
    Inferred: weight-reduction targets cited in the 2025 joint advisory. Not tested with this compound.
    Downside
    Excess protein beyond muscle needs isn't stored as muscle.

    Mechanistic support

    Complementary interventions

    A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

    Redundancies & overlap

    Actual endurance trainingThe thing it mimics. It's proven in humans; the compound isn't.
    5-Amino-1MQAlso sold for energy expenditure; preclinical only.

    Specific cautions

    • ADMSO as an injection solvent: the only approved DMSO medical product is for bladder instillation, not injection. Retail DMSO isn't made for parenteral use.3
    • DERRs regulate broad transcription programs. The off-target and long-term effects of activating them in humans are unknown.1
    • N/ENo human safety data at any dose.1

    Research gaps

    • Any human pharmacokinetics, safety or efficacy.
    • Solubility and stability of the catalog's BAC/DMSO mix.
    • Primary confirmation of the 12% mouse weight-loss figure.
