hCG

Human chorionic gonadotropin (LH-receptor agonist)

Catalog: HCG · Hormone and reproductive signaling · Evidence ceiling A

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • BMimics LH: stimulates Leydig cells to make testosterone and keeps intratesticular testosterone up. That's why it's used alongside testosterone therapy to preserve testicular function.2

Identity

AliasesNovarel, Pregnyl, chorionic gonadotropin (approved products)
ClassGlycoprotein hormone; acts on the LH/hCG receptor
TargetsLH receptor on testicular Leydig cells and ovarian cells
Regulatory statusFDA-approved: prepubertal cryptorchidism, male hypogonadotropic hypogonadism, ovulation induction. The catalog powder is not these products.
Supplier formulationPowder; 1,000 IU and 5,000 IU vials (catalog).

Intended effects

  • AMale hypogonadotropic hypogonadism1
  • BMaintaining intratesticular testosterone during exogenous testosterone2
  • AOvulation induction (with menotropins)1

Secondary findings

  • AThe label states hCG has NOT been shown to work for obesity: no added weight loss beyond calorie restriction.1

Human evidence

  • ALabel, male hypogonadotropic hypogonadism: 500–1,000 USP units 3×/week for 3 weeks, then the same dose 2×/week for 3 weeks (one of several regimens).1
  • BCoviello 2005 (29 healthy men on testosterone enanthate 200 mg/week): hCG 125, 250 or 500 IU every other day kept intratesticular testosterone up in a dose-related way. Low doses kept it in the normal range, vs near zero without hCG.2

    E Catalog protocols (researchdosing.com)3

    • Low dose: 250–500 IU 3×/week (1,000 IU vial + 1 mL)
    • Standard: 500 IU 3 days/week for 12 weeks (5,000 IU vial + 2 mL)
    • High dose: 1,500 → 2,000 → 1,000 IU 3×/week over 12 weeks
    • Morning; 8–16 week protocol, then 8–16 week washout

    Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

    Optimization & support

    Foundational support

    B150–300 min/wk moderate aerobic + strength work ≥2 days/wk4
    Why
    The proven fat-loss and metabolic-health lever, whatever compound is added.
    Mechanism
    Raises energy expenditure, improves insulin sensitivity and preserves lean mass.
    Basis
    General US guideline, not tested with this compound. Any 'exercise-mimetic' claim for a compound is no substitute.
    Downside
    None specific.

    Mechanistic support

    Complementary interventions

    BExogenous testosterone (TRT) + low-dose hCG2
    Why
    Testosterone suppresses LH, so intratesticular testosterone and sperm production fall. hCG replaces the LH signal.
    Mechanism
    LH-receptor agonism on Leydig cells.
    Basis
    Demonstrated for intratesticular testosterone (Coviello 2005). Fertility outcomes weren't studied there.
    Downside
    Raises estradiol; gynecomastia is a listed adverse reaction.

    A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

    Redundancies & overlap

    KisspeptinActs upstream (GnRH). It raises LH itself rather than replacing it.

    Specific cautions

    • AContraindicated: precocious puberty, prostate cancer or other androgen-dependent tumors, pregnancy.1
    • AIn women (ovulation induction): ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome, multiple births, arterial thromboembolism.1
    • AAndrogen-driven fluid retention: caution with heart or kidney disease, epilepsy, migraine, asthma.1
    • AAdverse reactions: headache, irritability, depression, fatigue, edema, gynecomastia, injection-site pain.1

    Research gaps

    • Fertility (sperm) outcomes with long-term TRT + hCG at catalog doses.
    • The catalog's 'high dose' 12-week protocol.
