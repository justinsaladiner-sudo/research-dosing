What is BPC-157 / TB-500 / KPV / Larazotide (oral)?

BPC-157 / TB-500 / KPV / Larazotide (oral): gut-repair capsule, 500 mcg of each. Larazotide (an oral gut tight-junction regulator) reached phase 3 for celiac disease; the trial was discontinued in 2022 after an interim analysis showed it couldn't succeed.
