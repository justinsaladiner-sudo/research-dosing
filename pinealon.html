Pinealon

Khavinson tripeptide (Glu-Asp-Arg)

Catalog: Pinealon · Neuropeptides and cognitive references · Evidence ceiling D

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • DIn vitro: reduced reactive oxygen species and necrotic cell death in cultured neural and immune cells (2011). Proposed to interact with DNA (2019 biophysics study).1

Identity

AliasesEDR peptide
ClassSynthetic tripeptide 'bioregulator' (Khavinson group, Russia)
Regulatory statusNot approved; no registered human trials.
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 20 mg vial (catalog).

Intended effects

  • ENeuroprotection, cognition, circadian rhythm (community use)2

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • CThe strongest human data is reportedly one open-label cohort (32 people) from the originating lab, with no placebo or blinding. The primary source wasn't found.1

    Preclinical findings

    • DCell-culture antioxidant and anti-necrotic effects (Khavinson group).1

    Documented dosing & cycles

    E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)2

    • 20 mg + 2 mL BAC water
    • First cycle: 2 mg once daily for 10 days
    • Later cycles: 2–5 mg daily for 10 days
    • 2–3 cycles per year

    Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

    Complementary interventions

    BMelatonin before bed3
    Why
    The evidence-backed sleep-onset aid, for compounds marketed for sleep.
    Mechanism
    MT1/MT2 receptor agonist; shifts and signals the circadian night.
    Basis
    Meta-analysis of 19 RCTs: falls asleep ~7 min faster; larger effects with higher dose and longer use. Not tested with DSIP, Pinealon or Epitalon.
    Downside
    Modest effect; next-day grogginess possible; stacking several sleep agents compounds sedation.

    A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

    Redundancies & overlap

    Epithalon and the other Khavinson bioregulatorsSame research group and concept; equally weak evidence.

    Specific cautions

    • N/ENo controlled human safety data.1

    Research gaps

    • The primary source for the reported 32-person cohort.
