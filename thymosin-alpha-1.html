Thymosin Alpha-1

Thymic immunomodulatory peptide

Catalog: Thymosin Alpha-1: TA1 · Immune and other signaling peptides · Evidence ceiling A

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • CEnhances cell-mediated immunity: promotes T-cell maturation and dendritic-cell activation via Toll-like receptors (mechanism per secondary reviews).1

Identity

AliasesThymalfasin, Tα1 · brand Zadaxin
Class28-amino-acid thymic peptide
TargetsToll-like receptor signaling in dendritic cells; T-cell maturation
Regulatory statusApproved as Zadaxin in about 35 countries (e.g. China, Italy) for chronic hepatitis B (and C in some). Not FDA- or EMA-approved.
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 10 mg vial (catalog).

Intended effects

  • AChronic hepatitis B (where approved)1
  • EGeneral immune support (community use)2

Secondary findings

  • AA transient ALT rise above twice baseline can occur during therapy. The label says to generally continue unless liver failure develops.1

Human evidence

  • AHepatitis B: 1.6 mg SC twice weekly for 6 months gave a significantly higher sustained response than untreated controls (pooled trials).1

    E Catalog protocols (researchdosing.com)2

    • 10 mg vial + 2 mL BAC water
    • 1.6 mg twice a week, 3–4 days apart
    • Or 1.6 mg daily for two weeks
    • Night preferred; 4–24 week cycle, then 2–12 week washout

    Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

    Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

    • The catalog's twice-weekly 1.6 mg matches the label regimen.

    Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

    Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.
