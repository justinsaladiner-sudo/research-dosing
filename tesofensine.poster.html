What is Tesofensine?

Tesofensine: triple monoamine (dopamine / noradrenaline / serotonin) reuptake inhibitor. Phase 2 (Astrup 2008, 203 adults, 24 wk): placebo-subtracted weight loss 4.5% / 9.2% / 10.6% at 0.25 / 0.5 / 1 mg. The Lancet issued an expression of concern on the paper in 2013.
