08

5-Amino-1MQ

NNMT inhibitor (small molecule)

Catalog: 5-Amino-1MQ · Metabolic compounds · Evidence ceiling D

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • DNNMT methylates nicotinamide using SAM. Blocking it is proposed to raise NAD+ and SAM in fat cells and increase energy expenditure.1
  • DNNMT knockdown in mice protected against diet-induced obesity. That's the validation of the target, not of 5-Amino-1MQ itself.1

Identity

Aliases5-amino-1-methylquinolinium
ClassSmall-molecule nicotinamide N-methyltransferase (NNMT) inhibitor. Not a peptide.
TargetsNNMT (adipose tissue and liver)
Regulatory statusResearch chemical; no human trials found.
Supplier formulationPowder for injection; 10 mg and 50 mg vials (catalog). The catalog also sells an oral form.

Intended effects

  • DFat loss / body recomposition2

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • N/ENo published human trials found. All human 'evidence' is vendor or anecdotal.2

    Preclinical findings

    • DDiet-induced obese mice: progressive weight loss over 11 days at 20 mg/kg three times daily (patent data).2
    • DVendor-cited mouse papers report smaller fat cells and less fat mass without reduced food intake or lean mass. These weren't verified against the journals.2

    Documented dosing & cycles

    E Catalog protocols (researchdosing.com)3

    • Daily dosing; 4–8 week cycles, then 2–4 week washout
    • 10 mg vial + 2 mL: 1 mg/day (weeks 1–4), then 2 mg/day or stay at 1 mg
    • Aggressive ladder: up to 5 mg/day by weeks 7–8
    • Morning or afternoon; a light meal may reduce GI effects

    Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

    Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

    • The aggressive ladder overlaps: 'Week 2–3: 2 mg' and 'Weeks 3–4: 3 mg' both claim week 3.
    • At 5 mg/day a 10 mg vial lasts 2 days.
    • Unit math: every unit value the catalog prints matches (10 mg/2 mL = 5 mg/mL; 50 mg/3 mL = 16.7 mg/mL).

    Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

    Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.

    Optimization & support

    Foundational support

    B150–300 min/wk moderate aerobic + strength work ≥2 days/wk4
    Why
    The proven fat-loss and metabolic-health lever, whatever compound is added.
    Mechanism
    Raises energy expenditure, improves insulin sensitivity and preserves lean mass.
    Basis
    General US guideline, not tested with this compound. Any 'exercise-mimetic' claim for a compound is no substitute.
    Downside
    None specific.
    CProtein 1.2–1.6 g/kg/d during active weight reduction5
    Why
    Protects lean mass during an energy deficit.
    Mechanism
    Supplies amino acids for muscle protein synthesis. Base the target on adjusted body weight or fat-free mass. Avoid ≥2 g/kg/d long term.
    Basis
    Inferred: weight-reduction targets cited in the 2025 joint advisory. Not tested with this compound.
    Downside
    Excess protein beyond muscle needs isn't stored as muscle.

    Mechanistic support

    Complementary interventions

    A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

    Redundancies & overlap

    NAD+ / NAD precursorsOverlap in the proposed NAD+-raising purpose (hypothesis only).
    SLU-PP-332Also sold as an energy-expenditure compound; preclinical only.

    Specific cautions

    • N/ENo human safety data at any dose.2
    • DNNMT also clears excess nicotinamide and affects methylation balance. The effects of inhibiting it long term in humans are unknown.1

    Research gaps

    • Any human pharmacokinetics, safety or efficacy.
    • Injectable vs oral exposure.
    • Primary verification of the vendor-cited mouse papers.
