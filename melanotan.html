Melanotan II

Non-selective melanocortin agonist

Catalog: Melanotan 2- MT2 · Immune and other signaling peptides · Evidence ceiling C

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • CMC1R activation darkens skin (tanning without UV); MC4R activation drives erections and libido and suppresses appetite. Non-selectivity is why side effects are broad.1

Identity

AliasesMT-II, MT2
ClassCyclic α-MSH analog; non-selective melanocortin receptor agonist
TargetsMC1R (skin pigment), MC3R/MC4R (libido, appetite)
Regulatory statusNot approved anywhere. Its relative bremelanotide (PT-141) is.
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 10 mg vial (catalog). Gray-market vials tested at 4.3–8.8 mg against a 10 mg label.

Intended effects

  • ETanning, libido (community use)3

Secondary findings

  • CNew moles, darkening of existing moles, atypical nevi reported.1

Human evidence

  • CSmall early human studies reported tanning and erections, with dose-limiting nausea and flushing (secondary summaries; original trials not retrieved).1
  • CCase reports: ischemic priapism needing surgery (2021 Sexual Medicine report, the third in the literature), plus reported melanoma, rhabdomyolysis, renal infarction and PRES.2
  • N/ENo completed human safety or efficacy trial has been published.1

Preclinical findings

    Documented dosing & cycles

    E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)3

    • 10 mg vial + 2.5 mL BAC water
    • 250 → 500 → 750 mcg (weeks 1–3), then 1,000 mcg daily (weeks 4–8)
    • Maintenance 500–1,000 mcg 1–2×/week (weeks 9–16)
    • 8–16 week cycle, then 8–16 week washout

    Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

    Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

    • Catalog: '28 units (1,000 mcg)'. At 4 mg/mL, 1,000 mcg is 25 units; 28 units is 1,120 mcg (12% over).
    • 500 mcg = 12.5 units; the catalog prints 13 (rounding).

    Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

    Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.

    Downside
    None.

    Complementary interventions

    A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

    Redundancies & overlap

    Bremelanotide (PT-141)Same family; approved, studied, MC4R-focused. The evidence-backed option for libido.

    Specific cautions

    • CMelanoma and atypical-mole reports: skin surveillance matters.1
    • CPriapism, sometimes requiring surgery.2
    • CRhabdomyolysis and renal infarction case reports.1
    • CGray-market vials can be underfilled (4.3–8.8 mg in '10 mg').1

    Research gaps

    • Any controlled human safety trial.
    • The original Dorr/Wessells trial data (not retrieved).
