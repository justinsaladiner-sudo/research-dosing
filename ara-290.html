ARA-290 (Cibinetide)

EPO-derived innate repair receptor agonist

Catalog: ARA-290 · Immune and other signaling peptides · Evidence ceiling B

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • BActivates the tissue-protective innate repair receptor: anti-inflammatory and pro-repair signaling, without EPO's blood-thickening effect.2

Identity

AliasesCibinetide (Araim Pharmaceuticals)
Class11-amino-acid peptide from erythropoietin's helix B; no red-cell-stimulating activity
TargetsInnate repair receptor (EPO receptor / CD131 heterocomplex)
Regulatory statusInvestigational; no approved indication. Clinical status listed as inactive (the developer closed).
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 16 mg vial (catalog).

Intended effects

  • BSmall-fiber neuropathy / neuropathic pain (sarcoidosis, diabetes)1

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • BPhase 2 (sarcoidosis small-fiber neuropathy, SC daily × 28 days): corneal and skin small-nerve-fiber counts increased (a possible disease-modifying sign). Pain improved in all groups; the placebo-corrected pain benefit at 4 mg wasn't significant (p=0.157).1
    • BEarlier study: improved symptoms and corneal nerve fiber density in sarcoidosis small-fiber loss.2
    • BNo phase 3 trials were completed.1

    Preclinical findings

      Documented dosing & cycles

      B Study regimen (phase 2)1

      4 mg SCDaily × 28 days

      28-day course. The 4 mg dose comes from secondary summaries of the trial; the protocol confirms daily SC dosing for 28 days.

      E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)3

      • 16 mg vial + 1.5 mL BAC water
      • 2, 3 or 4 mg once daily
      • Or 8 mg daily for at most 4 weeks
      • 4, 8 or 12 week cycles, then 2, 4 or 6 week washout

      Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

      Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

      • 2 mg = 18.75 units exactly; the catalog prints 18 (rounding).
      • Unit math otherwise matches (10.7 mg/mL).

      Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

      Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.
