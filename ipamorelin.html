Ipamorelin

Selective ghrelin-receptor (GHS-R) agonist

Catalog: Ipamorelin · Growth hormone axis · Evidence ceiling B

A Approved clinical use   B Human interventional   C Human observational / expert   D Preclinical   E Community / vendor   N/E Insufficient evidence

Mechanism

  • DActivates the ghrelin receptor, which triggers a GH pulse. It's marketed as 'selective' (little cortisol/prolactin effect); that claim comes from early animal pharmacology not re-verified here.1
  • BGhrelin-receptor agonism also speeds gastric emptying, the reason it was tested for postoperative ileus.1

Identity

AliasesNNC 26-0161
ClassPentapeptide growth-hormone secretagogue (ghrelin mimetic)
TargetsGHS-R1a (ghrelin receptor) on the pituitary and gut
Regulatory statusNever approved. Phase 2 for postoperative ileus did not meet its primary endpoint.
Supplier formulationLyophilized research powder; 10 mg vial (catalog).

Intended effects

  • BPostoperative ileus (tested; failed)1
  • EGH elevation for body composition, recovery, sleep (community use)2

Secondary findings

    Human evidence

    • BPhase 2 (n=114 bowel-resection patients, dosed from postoperative day 1 to day 7): time to a solid meal 25.3 h vs 32.6 h placebo, not significant (p=0.15). Adverse events were similar (87.5% vs 94.8%).1
    • N/ENo human trials of subcutaneous ipamorelin for body composition.1

    Preclinical findings

    • DRat postoperative ileus model: faster gastric emptying vs vehicle.1

    Documented dosing & cycles

    E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)2

    • 100–300 mcg per dose, 1–3 times a day
    • Bedtime; add on waking; add within 30 min post-workout
    • Fasted (≥1.5 h before/after a meal)
    • 8–12 week cycle, then 4–6 week washout

    Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

    Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

    • 100 mcg = 2.5 units and 300 mcg = 7.5 units; the catalog prints 3 and 8 (rounding).
    • Unit math matches (4 mg/mL).

    Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

    Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.

    Optimization & support

    Foundational support

    BProtect slow-wave sleep4
    Why
    The largest natural GH pulse comes in early slow-wave sleep. That's the physiology bedtime GH-secretagogue dosing tries to ride.
    Mechanism
    Slow-wave sleep and nocturnal GH release are tightly coupled. Both decline with age.
    Basis
    Inferred: GH physiology. Not tested as an add-on to this compound.
    Downside
    None.
    B150–300 min/wk moderate aerobic + strength work ≥2 days/wk5
    Why
    The proven fat-loss and metabolic-health lever, whatever compound is added.
    Mechanism
    Raises energy expenditure, improves insulin sensitivity and preserves lean mass.
    Basis
    General US guideline, not tested with this compound. Any 'exercise-mimetic' claim for a compound is no substitute.
    Downside
    None specific.

    Mechanistic support

    BDose away from carbohydrate / high-glucose meals6
    Why
    Raised glucose and insulin blunt GH release, which is why the catalog says to dose fasted.
    Mechanism
    Oral glucose suppresses GH secretion; acromegaly is diagnosed by the absence of that suppression.
    Basis
    Inferred: GH physiology. The catalog's specific fasting windows are untested.
    Downside
    Hypoglycemia risk if combined with insulin-lowering drugs while fasted.
    AMonitor fasting glucose / HbA1c3
    Why
    GH and IGF-1 raise insulin resistance.
    Mechanism
    GH counters insulin action. In tesamorelin trials HbA1c ≥6.5% developed in 5% vs 1% on placebo.
    Basis
    Demonstrated for tesamorelin (label); applied by analogy to other GH secretagogues.
    Downside
    None.

    Complementary interventions

    A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.

    Redundancies & overlap

    GHRP-2, GHRP-6Same ghrelin receptor; less selective (more appetite, cortisol, prolactin).
    CJC-1295 no DAC, sermorelin, tesamorelinDifferent receptor (GHRH). The usual catalog pairing; no outcome trial tests it.

    Specific cautions

    • ARaised GH/IGF-1: glucose intolerance, fluid retention (class analogy).3
    • N/ENo long-term human safety data for repeated SC dosing.1

    Research gaps

    • Any body-composition trial in humans.
    • Human confirmation of the 'selective' (no cortisol/prolactin) claim at catalog doses.
