      Documented dosing & cycles

      E Catalog protocol (researchdosing.com)1

      • 12.5 / 2.5 mg + 1.5 mL BAC water
      • Weeks 1–4: 12 units (1 mg retatrutide + 200 mcg cagrilintide) once a week
      • Weeks 5–8: 36 units (3 mg + 600 mcg) once a week
      • Only for people new to retatrutide; catalog says cagrilintide can't be run indefinitely (antibody claim)

      Preserved from the catalog as written. This is a vendor protocol, not a clinical recommendation.

      Catalog discrepancies (flagged, record unchanged)

      • Unit math matches (8.33 mg/mL retatrutide; 1.67 mg/mL cagrilintide).
      • The 1 mg retatrutide start is below the 2 mg trial start.
      • The catalog's anti-cagrilintide antibody claim wasn't verified.

      Formulation math: U-100 syringe units

      Units = dose ÷ concentration × 100 (100 units = 1 mL). "Catalog" = what researchdosing.com prints; ⚠ = off by more than rounding (0.6 units or 5%). Over 100 units = split the injection. The math says nothing about sterility, compatibility or stability after mixing.

      Complementary interventions

      BCreatine monohydrate with resistance training3
      Why
      Helps protect lean mass while drug-driven weight loss strips muscle as well as fat.
      Mechanism
      Raises muscle creatine stores, supporting training volume and lean-mass gains.
      Basis
      Strong evidence for lean mass with resistance training (ISSN 2017). Not tested together with GLP-1 drugs; inferred.
      Downside
      Water weight gain (scale noise); caution with existing kidney disease.

      A plausible mechanism doesn't prove synergy. Nothing here is called synergistic without combination data.
